Master UI has one material language. Light mode is **moonstone**, a clean zinc pearl. Dark mode is **obsidian**, a matte zinc glass whose surfaces step apart and keep a legible edge. Both are lit by one source above and slightly to the left, so every raised face catches a line of light on its top edge and settles on a soft, converged shadow.

The character is restrained: ink rather than color, hairline contours, small radii and depth that changes with state. Detail comes from light, not decoration.

## Principles \{#principles\}

1. **Monochrome interface.** Controls use ink and pearl only. Hue marks status, always beside words that say the same thing; gold is kept for one brand moment per view.
2. **Zinc neutrals.** Every neutral is the preset's zinc, with a hue of 265 at almost no chroma. Lines and shadows carry no hue of their own.
3. **One material ladder.** Base, tray, raised, floating; an inset sits below its surroundings. Never invent a surface color.
4. **State is depth.** A control lifts by half a pixel on hover and sinks into a well when pressed. Do not express state by changing hue.
5. **Small concentric radii.** Radii nest by a 4px inset, so an outer corner is the inner one plus the space between them.
6. **Converged shadows.** Every shadow layer doubles its offset and blur and pulls its spread in by half the offset. There is no single large blur.

## Surfaces \{#surfaces\}

Surfaces form one ladder. Each step is a role, not a color: pick the step by what the surface does, and the theme resolves its value in light and dark.

| Token | Role | Moonstone | Obsidian |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `--color-surface-base` | The page canvas | 98.6% | 19.4% |
| `--color-surface-tray` | A recessed holder for sheets | 96% | 17.4% |
| `--color-surface-raised` | A raised face: cards, buttons | 100% | 24.2% |
| `--color-surface-raised-low` | The lower end of a raised face's gradient | 99.3% | 22.8% |
| `--color-surface-inset` | A well: fields, tracks | 96.6% | 16.4% |
| `--color-surface-floating` | Popovers and menus over content | 100% | 26.4% |
| `--color-surface-chip` | Neutral chips and badges | 96.5% | white 7% |
| `--color-surface-inverse` | Ink: primary buttons, checked controls | 22% | 95% |

Values are OKLCH lightness. A raised face is a top-to-bottom gradient from `raised` to `raised-low`, which `@apply --surface-sheet` writes for you.

## Lines \{#lines\}

Lines are neutral ink alphas on moonstone and white alphas on obsidian. Each dark step is a little firmer than its light twin, so dark surfaces never blur into each other.

| Token | Moonstone | Obsidian | Use for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `--color-line-hair` | 6.5% | 7.5% | Quiet separators inside a surface |
| `--color-line-contour` | 9.5% | 11% | The outline of every surface |
| `--color-line-divider` | 12% | 13% | Rules between sections, tab rows |
| `--color-line-strong` | 20% | 22% | Hovered contours, control borders |
| `--color-line-mark` | 30% | 32% | A focused field's contour |

## Elevation \{#elevation\}

Elevation is the order of depth, from a pressed well to an overlay. A raised face combines an inset highlight on its top edge, one contour and three to six falling shades.

The layers converge: each doubles the previous offset and blur, and its negative spread is half its offset. A shadow therefore stays tight under the object instead of spreading into a grey halo.

## Shape \{#shape\}

Radii are small and nest by a 4px inset: a 14px tray around a 10px sheet, a 9px segmented track around 6px thumbs.

| Radius | Used by |
| --- | --- |
| 3px | Keycaps |
| 4px (`--radius-sm`) | Badges, checkboxes |
| 6px (`--radius-md`) | Buttons, fields, segmented thumbs |
| 10px | Sheets |
| 14px | Trays |

Only pills and dots are rounder.

## Type \{#type\}

Components inherit the page's font family. Master UI is designed with [Geist](https://vercel.com/font) and its stylistic sets, at a light regular weight for running text:

```css name=app.css
@theme {
  --font-sans: 'Geist';
  --font-feature-sans: 'ss02', 'ss03', 'ss04', 'ss06', 'ss07', 'ss08';
}

body {
  font-weight: 350;
}
```

Control labels use `--font-weight-control` (460), a light medium that reads clearly without looking heavy. Headings stay regular: size and ink carry the steps.

| --- | --- | --- |

| `--duration-press` | 60ms | Pressing a control |
| `--duration-quick` | 160ms | Hover and focus |
| `--duration-base` | 280ms | Thumbs and panels that move |

## Color schemes \{#color-schemes\}

Every color token resolves with `light-dark()`, so the theme follows the element's `color-scheme`. The Master CSS preset sets `color-scheme: light dark` on the root and lets you pin a scheme for the page or for one subtree:

```html
<html data-theme="dark">
  …
  <aside data-theme="light">A moonstone panel on an obsidian page</aside>
</html>
```

There is no second stylesheet for dark mode and no dark-only class on components.

## Customize \{#customize\}

Override tokens after importing Master UI. Components read them through `var()`, so one change reaches every component:

```css name=app.css
@import '@master/css';
@import '@master/ui';

@theme {
  /* Rounder controls */
  --radius-md: .5rem;
  /* A warmer canvas */
  --color-surface-base: light-dark(oklch(98.4% .004 80), oklch(19.4% .004 60));
}
```

Because the tokens live in `@theme`, they are also utilities: `bg-surface-tray`, `shadow-lifted` and `b-line-contour` paint custom elements with the same materials.

Importing Master UI tunes the preset's semantic tokens, such as `--color-surface-raised`, `--color-line-divider` and `--color-text-body`, to moonstone and obsidian, so the rest of your markup matches the components.
