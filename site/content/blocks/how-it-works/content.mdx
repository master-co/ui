A "how it works" block walks a visitor through what they will do, in the order they will do it, before they are asked to start. It is an ordered list set as a grid: each step has its number in words, a title, one sentence, and a small [sheet](/components/surface#sheet) that shows what the step leaves behind. The row of sheets is the argument: read across it and the product has run once.

Blocks are markup to copy. They take the page's own text color and size; the previews set `fg-text-body text-sm` on the body. Each block is a size container, so it reflows by its own width: in a wide page, a sidebar's worth of room or a dialog.

## What is in it \{#anatomy\}

- **The steps are an `<ol>`.** The order is the content, so the element says it: a screen reader announces "list, 3 items" and each step's place. The list is a grid, one column that becomes three with `grid-cols(3)@container(--4xl)`. Each item is a grid too, which draws no marker, so the browser's own numbers are not printed beside the ones written in words.
- **The number is an [eyebrow](/components/heading#eyebrow-and-lead), "Step 1".** It is a `<p>` over the step's `<h3>`, as the block's own eyebrow is over its title. A numeral drawn in a circle would be one more shape to invent, and a number written into the `<h3>` would make a list of headings read "1 Connect a repository".
- **Each step shows what it leaves behind.** A small `sheet` holds one line: the repository and the framework that was found, the command and the build it started, the address and how long it took. The words name what is there, and a [badge](/components/badge) says its state. In three columns they stand over the text, as a picture stands over a card's title, so the three line up whatever the length of the sentences. Between a phone and that width a step is a row instead, its sheet beside its words: a line of text across a tablet is too long to read, and a sheet that wide is mostly empty.
- **The states are statuses, so they may take a hue.** "Building" is the info tone and "Ready in 48s" the success tone, each with a dot and each saying in words what the tone says. The framework's name is no status and stays neutral.
- **A step is a title and one sentence.** Three steps is a promise that it is simple. If the honest count is seven, this block is the wrong one: that is documentation.
- **The ask comes after the steps**, not before: "Start deploying" is the page's one `btn-brand`, with the way to read more beside it.

## Accessibility \{#accessibility\}

- The block is a `<section>` named by its `<h2>`. The steps are `<h3>`, one level down.
- Keep the `<ol>`. Nothing here sets `list-style: none`, which in Safari would take the list's role away along with its markers.
- The line in each sheet is an illustration of the step and is read with it, before the step's title. Keep it text: a screenshot in its place needs an `alt` that says what the step produced.
- A badge's tone is never the only signal. The words "Building" and "Ready in 48s" carry the state in forced colors and in a monochrome print.
