An activity feed says what happened in a workspace and who did it: deployments, comments, files. It is a [timeline](/components/timeline) for each day, newest first, with the person as the marker and what they touched in the words. A switch narrows it to one kind of event.

<PagePreview name="default" title="Activity feed" example="activity-feed" touchHeight={{ desktop: 1028, tablet: 1028, phone: 1290 }} caption="Choose a kind of event to narrow the feed, or open a deployment's details." />

Blocks are markup to copy. They take the page's own text color and size; the previews set `fg-text-body text-sm` on the body. Each block is a size container, so it reflows by its own width: in a wide page, a sidebar's worth of room or a dialog.

## What is in it \{#anatomy\}

- **One [sheet](/components/surface#sheet)**, its header and its days parted by a hairline, `bt(1) bt-solid b-line-hair`.
- **A day is a heading and a timeline.** Today, Yesterday, then dates, each an `<h3>` over an `<ol class="timeline">`, newest first. The count in the header says how many events are shown, and agrees with the days under it.
- **The person is the marker.** An [avatar](/components/avatar) in `timeline-marker` takes the place of the dot, at the marker's 24px; the line joins one person to the next. The name is in the sentence, so a photo's `alt` is empty and initials are `aria-hidden`.
- **What it touched is a link.** The deployment, the preview or the issue in the title is a [link](/components/link) to it; files are [files](/components/file) a reader opens.
- **Words keep a measure.** A quoted comment is `max-w(560)` and the files and the details `max-w(420)`, so a wide sheet does not stretch a sentence across itself; the rows are as wide as the sheet, the reading is not.
- **A status is a tone, a project is a category.** "Ready" and "Failed" are status [badges](/components/badge) with a dot and the word; the project an event belongs to is `badge-category` with its name, in the [category](/components/category) the project has everywhere else.
- **The newest event is current.** `aria-current="true"` on its row takes the marker's ink, when the marker is a dot.
- **Details stay folded.** The build of a deployment and the reason one failed are in an [expand](/components/expand): its first lines show, and the native `<details>` opens the rest, with find-in-page and the keyboard as they are.
- **The switch is a [segmented](/components/segmented) control.** It narrows the feed to deployments, comments or files; a day left with none leaves the list.

## What needs a script \{#script\}

The feed is markup the server writes, and reads in full without a script. `main.js` only narrows it: it keeps the events of the chosen kind in their lists, takes out a day that is left with none, and updates the count, which is a polite live region. A server that pages the feed answers the same switch as a query.

## Accessibility \{#accessibility\}

- The sheet is a `<section>` named by its heading; each day is a `<section>` named by its own.
- Each event is one sentence that says who did what to which, so it reads without the avatar, the badges or the line.
- A status badge says the status in words; a category badge says the project's name, and its color only repeats it.
- Times are `<time datetime>` elements, so a reader gets the exact moment and a program can read it.
- The switch is a radio group named "Show"; the count after a choice is announced.
