A site header holds the brand, the main links and the one thing you most want a visitor to do. This block is a [navbar](/components/navbar) with everything a real site adds to it: a menu under one of the links, a second action that steps aside when the room is short, and a drawer that takes over on a phone. None of it needs a script.

## What is in it \{#anatomy\}

- **The bar is a [navbar](/components/navbar).** It sticks, and takes its frosted material only once content is under it.
- **A link that opens a menu is a button.** `navbar-link` is on links and on one `<button popovertarget>`: Product opens something, the others go somewhere.
- **The product menu is a [popover](/components/popover) holding a [list](/components/list).** It anchors to its button and flips at the edge of the viewport. Each row is a link with a title and one sentence.
- **Two actions, one of which steps aside.** Both are [buttons](/components/button) on links. "Sign in" is `hidden inline-flex@container(--xl)`: it shows from 36rem of bar. "Start deploying" is the one `btn-primary`, and it always stays.
- **The toggle exists only while the links are hidden.** `navbar-toggle` shows below 36rem of bar; that threshold belongs to the navbar.
- **The drawer is a [dialog](/components/dialog#drawer) holding a [nav](/components/nav).** `commandfor` opens it; its close button, a click outside or <kbd>Esc</kbd> closes it. It holds the same links as the bar, and both actions in its footer.

The bar is the size container here, so `@container(…)` utilities inside it measure the bar.

## Accessibility \{#accessibility\}

- The first element of the page is a [skip link](/components/skip) to `<main>`, which has `tabindex="-1"` so focus moves with the jump. The header is what it skips.
- The header is a `<header>`; its links are in a `<nav>` with a label. The drawer holds a second `<nav>` with a different label.
- The product link is a button: it opens something instead of going somewhere. The popover it opens is a list of links, reachable with Tab and closed with Escape.
- Mark the current page with `aria-current="page"` on its link, in the bar and in the drawer.
- Set `scroll-padding-top: 3.5rem` on the root, so a link to a heading does not land under the bar.
