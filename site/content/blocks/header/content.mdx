A site header holds the brand, the main links and the one thing you most want a visitor to do. This block is a [navbar](/components/navbar) with everything a real site adds to it: a menu under one of the links, a second action that steps aside when the room is short, and a drawer that takes over on a phone. None of it needs a script.

## What is in it \{#anatomy\}

| Piece | Built with | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The bar | [`navbar`](/components/navbar) | Sticky, and frosted only once content is under it |
| The links | `navbar-link` on links and on a button | A link that opens a menu is a `<button popovertarget>` |
| The product menu | [`popover`](/components/popover) holding a [`list`](/components/list) | It anchors to its button and flips at the edge |
| The actions | [`btn`](/components/button) on links | "Sign in" is `hidden inline-flex@container(--xl)`: it shows from 36rem of bar |
| The toggle | `navbar-toggle` | It exists only while the links are hidden |
| The drawer | [`dialog dialog-drawer`](/components/dialog#drawer) holding a [`nav`](/components/nav) | Opened by `commandfor`, closed by a click outside or Escape |

The bar is the size container here, so `@container(…)` utilities inside it measure the bar. The links leave below 36rem of bar; that threshold belongs to the navbar.

## Accessibility \{#accessibility\}

- The header is a `<header>`; its links are in a `<nav>` with a label. The drawer holds a second `<nav>` with a different label.
- The product link is a button: it opens something instead of going somewhere. The popover it opens is a list of links, reachable with Tab and closed with Escape.
- Mark the current page with `aria-current="page"` on its link, in the bar and in the drawer.
- Set `scroll-padding-top: 3.5rem` on the root, so a link to a heading does not land under the bar.
