Blocks are markup to copy. They take the page's own text color and size; the previews set `fg-text-body text-sm` on the body. Each block is a size container, so it reflows by its own width: in a wide page, a sidebar's worth of room or a dialog.

## What is in it \{#anatomy\}

- **Search address** The GET form stores the query in the URL. Point its action at the route that renders the block in your project.
- **Article and section links** An article title opens its guide. The links below use stable section IDs. A title match keeps the guide’s sections visible.

## Accessibility \{#accessibility\}

Keep the search field labelled and the links in document order. The result count and the empty message remain readable without relying on the highlighted text.
