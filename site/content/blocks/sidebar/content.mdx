The block is everything inside `<aside class="shell-sidebar">`. The shell decides where the sidebar stands, how tall it is and when it steps aside; the block decides what is in it.

## What is in it \{#anatomy\}

- **The workspace is a quiet [button](/components/button) that opens a [menu](/components/menu).** `justify-start` sets its content at the start and `ml-auto` pushes the mark to the end. The letter beside the name is an [avatar](/components/avatar), hidden from assistive technology since the name says it.
- **Search is a small [input group](/components/input#input-group).** The shortcut is two [keycaps](/components/kbd), one to a key; wire it to a [command palette](/components/command).
- **The navigation is a [nav](/components/nav)** with icons, a count, a group and a label. A group is a native `<details>`: it opens without script.
- **Usage is a [well](/components/surface#well) with a [meter](/components/meter).** One thing worth watching, the plan's build minutes, and a [link](/components/link) to change it.
- **The account is a quiet button that opens a menu above itself**, `menu-top`. It is the last thing in the column, pushed down by `mt-auto`.
- **One column, one gap.** The sidebar is a flex column; `gap-sm` separates its pieces and `p-sm` holds them off the edge. `mt-auto` on the last group sends it to the foot.
- **The sidebar scrolls by itself** when the navigation is longer than the viewport; that comes from `shell-sidebar`.
- **No surface of its own.** It stands on the shell's tray. Its rows rise as small sheets when current, which is the nav's own doing.

## In the drawer \{#drawer\}

Below 48rem of shell the sidebar leaves the layout, and a block that only left would strand a phone. The example above keeps the way in: a button with `shell-toggle`, which exists only while the sidebar is away, opens a [drawer](/components/dialog#drawer) with `dialog-drawer-start` that holds the same navigation under a second label. See the [app shell](/pages/app-shell) for the frame. In the drawer, leave the search out: a phone has its own.

## Accessibility \{#accessibility\}

- The sidebar is an `<aside>`; the links are in a `<nav>` with a label. Mark the current page with `aria-current="page"`.
- The workspace and account buttons open menus of buttons and links. They are plain popovers: Tab moves through them and Escape closes them.
- The avatar beside the name is decoration, with an empty `alt`.
- The meter carries its value as text and is named by the label beside it.
- The drawer is a `<dialog>`: focus moves into it, its close button, a click outside or <kbd>Esc</kbd> closes it, and focus returns to Menu.
