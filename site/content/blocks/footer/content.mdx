A footer is where a site ends: columns of quiet links for the visitor who scrolled this far looking for something, and the small print. It has no material and no state, which is why it is a block and not a component. It is utilities, [quiet links](/components/link#quiet) and one hairline.

Blocks are markup to copy. They take the page's own text color and size; the previews set `fg-text-body text-sm` on the body. Each block is a size container, so it reflows by its own width.

## What is in it \{#anatomy\}

- **A hairline above**, `bt(1) bt-solid b-line-hair`, and muted ink on everything, `fg-text-muted`. The footer is the quietest thing on the page.
- **The brand and one sentence** at the start, with a status [badge](/components/badge) under them.
- **Four columns of links.** Two columns on a phone, four from 42rem of block: `grid-cols(2) grid-cols(4)@container(--2xl)`. From 56rem the brand takes one third of the row and the columns two.
- **`link link-quiet`.** A quiet link takes the color around it and shows its line only when touched, so forty links do not rule the page. `self-start` keeps each link as wide as its word, so the empty row beside it is not a target.
- **The small print** on its own row under a second hairline.

Each column is a `<nav>` named by its heading with `aria-labelledby`. The headings are `<h2>` at `heading-xs`: a footer stands outside every section of the page, so its columns are not under anything.

A footer is the one block that sets ink on its own root. A block takes the page's ink; this one is quieter than the page on purpose, and `fg-text-muted` on the `<footer>` is how.

## One line \{#simple\}

For an application, or a site with little to say at the foot, one row: the copyright at the start and a few links at the end. It wraps to two lines on a phone.

## Accessibility \{#accessibility\}

- The block is a `<footer>`. Outside `<main>` and any `<article>`, it is the page's content-info landmark; a page has one.
- Several `<nav>` elements need labels that differ; here each takes its column's heading.
- Muted ink on the base surface meets contrast for body text in both schemes; do not go lighter for "fine print".
