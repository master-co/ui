Blocks are markup to copy. They take the page's own text color and size; the previews set `fg-text-body text-sm` on the body. Each block is a size container, so it reflows by its own width: in a wide page, a sidebar's worth of room or a dialog. Center the card on its page with the [centered](/layouts/centered) layout.

## What is in it \{#anatomy\}

- **`sheet form w-full max-w(380) p-xl`.** The card fills a phone and stops at 380px. `form` sets the gap between everything in it.
- **The fast ways first.** A provider and a passkey are one click each; the password is the fallback. A [divider](/components/divider#label) with "or" parts the two.
- **"Forgot it?" beside the label**, not under the field. It is a `link-quiet` in muted ink at the end of the label's row, so it is found without competing with the button. The label points at its field with `for`, since the field is no longer inside it.
- **One primary button**, the last control, as wide as the fields.
- **The way to the other card** under it: one line, one link.

The fields carry `autocomplete` values, `username` and `current-password`, which is what lets a password manager fill them.

## Sign up \{#sign-up\}

The same card with more fields. The names share a row, `grid grid-cols(2) gap-sm`: two short fields that are one answer. A `form-row` would not split here, since it waits for 28rem of form and this card has 22. The password's rule is a `field-hint` tied to the input with `aria-describedby`, shown before the reader makes the mistake.

The `otp` [behavior](/guide/behaviors) moves focus from one slot to the next and spreads a pasted code across them; without it each slot is still a field that takes one digit. ## Accessibility \{#accessibility\}

- The card's title is the page's `<h1>`.
- Every field has a visible label. A placeholder is an example, not a name.
- Errors appear with the field they belong to: add a `field-error` and it shows while the field is invalid; see [input](/components/input#hint-and-error).
- Do not disable the button until the form is valid. A disabled button cannot say what is missing; a submitted form can.
- Keep "show password" in mind for long passwords: a quiet button inside an [input group](/components/input#input-group) that switches the field's `type`.
