Blocks are markup to copy. They take the page's own text color and size; the previews set `fg-text-body text-sm` on the body. Each block is a size container, so it reflows by its own width.

## What is in it \{#anatomy\}

- **A grid by the block's width.** One column, two from 42rem and three from 64rem: `grid-cols(2)@container(--2xl) grid-cols(3)@container(--5xl)`. Six services fill each of those without a card left alone on a row.
- **Each service is a `card` on a list item, and one link.** The name is an `<a class="card-link">` whose target covers the card; the sentence under it stands above that target and can be selected.
- **A letter mark, not a logo.** An `avatar avatar-square` holds two letters in the system's own ink and is `aria-hidden`, since the name stands beside it. Six brand marks in six colors would be the loudest thing on the page, and each comes with rules for its use. Where a product has the right to show a mark, it goes in the same square.
- **What kind of service it is** is a neutral [badge](/components/badge) in `card-action`: "Source", "Chat", "Monitoring". A visitor scanning for their issue tracker reads the badges first.
- **One sentence about the result**, not the mechanism: what the team sees once the two are connected.
- **A way in for what is not listed**, as one muted line under the grid.

## Connections \{#connections\}

Inside the product the same services are a list of connections in a settings page, and what matters is their state. Each row of the [list](/components/list) is a link to that integration's own page, where it is connected, changed or removed; the state is a badge at the end of the row.

- **Three states, each a word.** "Connected" in the success tone, "Reauthorize" in the warning tone, and a neutral "Not connected". The tone repeats the word; a service that was never connected is not a problem, so it takes none.
- **The line under a name is the fact that matters in that state**: the account it is connected to, what went wrong, or what kind of service it would be. It keeps one line, so it is a few words.
- **The whole row is the link.** `list list-interactive` makes each `<a class="list-row">` a rounded target, 44px tall at least on a touch screen. Connecting happens on the page the row leads to, where the service asks for its own permission; the list starts nothing by itself.
- **The header takes the rows' inset.** A row starts 18px in, the list's `p-xs` and its own .625rem, so the header is `px(18)` and the title starts where the marks do.

## Accessibility \{#accessibility\}

- Each block is a `<section>` named by its heading. In the grid each service's name is an `<h3>`.
- A card and a row are each one link, named by everything in it: "GitHub, atlas-labs, Connected". The state is part of the name, so nobody has to see the color.
- The letter marks and the chevrons are `aria-hidden`.
- A row that needs attention says so in its badge and in the line under its name, not by a hue alone.
- Do not open a permission window from a list of rows. A reader who presses "Slack" expects Slack's page, with what will be shared stated before anything is asked.
