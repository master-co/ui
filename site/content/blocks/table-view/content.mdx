A table view is a table with what it takes to work in one: a title and the main action, a filter and a sort, selection, an action on each row, and pages. Each of those is a component; the block is how they sit together on one sheet.

<PagePreview name="default" title="Table view" example="table-view" touchHeight={{ desktop: 860, tablet: 860, phone: 1064 }} caption="Search, filter, sort and page through the projects, or switch to the people and the files. Select rows to delete them." />

Blocks are markup to copy. They take the page's own text color and size; the previews set `fg-text-body text-sm` on the body. Each block is a size container, so it reflows by its own width: in a wide page, a sidebar's worth of room or a dialog.

## What is in it \{#anatomy\}

- **One [sheet](/components/surface#sheet).** The bands are parted by hairlines, `bt(1) bt-solid b-line-hair`, not by cards inside the card.
- **The header is a split.** The workspace and a count that says how many rows match, then a [segmented](/components/segmented) switch between projects, people and files and the primary action: `flex flex-wrap items-center justify-between`, so the switch and the action drop under the title when the sheet is narrow.
- **Search, filters and sort are one wrapping row.** A search [input group](/components/input#input-group) that grows from 200px to 320px, the [filter bar](/blocks/filter-bar)'s button, and a raised [select](/components/select) for the order, sent to the end with `ml-auto`. Applied filters take a line of their own under it, as [chips](/components/chip#values) that each remove one.
- **The order is shown where it is read.** The heading of the sorted column carries `aria-sort`, which the [table](/components/table#sorting) draws as strong ink and a chevron; the select is where it changes.
- **The table scrolls in its own band.** It stands in a [scroll area](/components/scroll-area) with `scroll-area-inline`, so a narrow sheet scrolls the table and not the page, and the edge fades where columns continue.
- **Each set has its own first cell.** A project is its name over its domain, a person an [avatar](/components/avatar) beside a name and an email, a file an [icon frame](/components/icon-frame) beside its name. Each is a `<th scope="row">`, so a cell is announced with its row.
- **Selection is of what matches.** `aria-selected="true"` on a `table-row` gives it the selected tone and its checkbox is checked. The header checkbox selects every row that matches the search and the filters, on every page, and is `indeterminate` when only some are; a row that stops matching leaves the selection, so "3 of 15 selected" never counts what cannot be seen.
- **The footer is a split.** The selection, with Delete and Clear selection while there is one, then the range and a [pagination](/components/pagination) of buttons. Previous and Next are disabled at the ends; many pages keep the first, the last and the neighbors of the current one, with a gap between.
- **Row actions hide in a [menu](/components/menu)** behind a quiet button at the end, named for its row: "Actions for Atlas".
- **Nothing matches, and it says so.** An [empty](/components/empty) state fills the body with a way back: clear the search and the filters.

## What needs a script \{#script\}

The rows are data, so the example's `main.js` writes them, from `data.js`, into templates the markup holds for each set: the stylesheet and the utilities come from the markup, the words from the data. It shares the [filter bar](/blocks/filter-bar)'s two modules: `query.js` searches, filters, sorts and pages plain rows, and `filters.js` runs the bar. In a product these questions go to your data source; the block shows how each answer is drawn. Without a script, wrap the bands in a `<form method="get">`: the search, the select and the checkboxes become a query string the server can answer, and the pagination becomes links.

## Acting on a selection \{#selection\}

The example keeps its one action for a selection in the footer, beside the count. When there are several, show them in a floating [toolbar](/components/toolbar#floating): the count, the actions, and a way to clear. Place it with `sticky bottom(16)` at the foot of the sheet, or fixed at the foot of the viewport; show it while the selection is not empty.

## Accessibility \{#accessibility\}

- The sheet is a `<section>` named by its title, and the table is named by it too.
- Every checkbox has a name that includes its row: "Select Atlas". The header's says what it selects: "Select all 15 projects".
- The count under the title is plain text; a status region, `sr-only`, says what an action did: "Page 2 of 3", "3 projects deleted". Focus stays on what was pressed, or moves to the header checkbox when the rows it stood in are gone.
- The actions column has a heading for assistive technology, visually hidden.
- The pagination is a `<nav>` with a label; its pages are buttons, since they change the view in place, and the current one carries `aria-current="page"`.
- The first cell of each row is a `<th scope="row">`, so a cell is announced with its project, person or file.
- The filters open in a popover named "Filter projects"; [the filter bar](/blocks/filter-bar#accessibility) says how its rows and its messages read.
- The table scrolls sideways in a scroll area. It holds checkboxes and buttons, so the keyboard reaches it through them; a table with nothing focusable in it needs `tabindex="0"`, a role and a name on the scroll area.
