A box plot sums a group of readings up in five figures so that several groups can be compared at once. The box runs from the first quartile to the third, a line of the surface cuts it at the median, and whiskers reach to the lowest and the highest reading within one and a half boxes of it. A reading further out is a dot of its own.

Each series is one group: give its raw readings as `values`, and its name stands under its box. The scale fits the readings and does not start at zero. The readout lists the five figures from the top, and `words` names them in the page's language: `{ min, q1, median, q3, max }`. With `horizontal` the boxes run across.

A box plot hides the shape between its five figures: two peaks look the same as one. Where the shape matters, draw a [histogram](/charts/histogram) of each group.

## Options \{#options\}

| Option | Default | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `label` | none | What the chart shows and in what unit: the plot's accessible name |
| `series` | `[]` | Each with a `name` and what its form reads: `values`, a `value`, `points`, `ranges` or `children`; optionally a `category`, a `type`, `low` and `high`, and a ring's `max` |
| `horizontal` | `false` | Bars, ranges, dumbbells and boxes run across |
| `words` | English | The names of a box plot's five figures: `min`, `q1`, `median`, `q3` and `max` |
| `min`, `max`, `ticks` | fit, `4` | The ends of the scale and about how many steps mark it; `xMin`, `xMax` and `xTicks` for a scatter's scale across |
| `format`, `tickFormat` | the locale's | How a value and a tick are written; `xFormat` for a scatter's scale across |
| `current` | none | The index of the point marked `aria-current` |

The readout, the keys, the legend, the colors and the scale are the same in every form: [Chart](/components/chart) holds them, with every option, class and custom property.
