A sunburst shows a whole in two levels. Each series is a slice of the inner ring, its share of the whole, and its `children` are the slices of the outer ring over it. So a reader sees the parts and what each is made of from the middle outward. A part's children take its color in lighter steps, and the middle is kept for the readout.

A part is the sum of its children, unless it gives a `value` of its own; a part with no children is a slice of the inner ring alone. The legend names the parts, and each child says its name in the readout and in its point's name. Keep to two levels and a few children each: a third ring is not read, and a [treemap](/charts/treemap) holds more parts.

## Options \{#options\}

| Option | Default | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `label` | none | What the chart shows and in what unit: the plot's accessible name |
| `series` | `[]` | Each with a `name` and what its form reads: `values`, a `value`, `points`, `ranges` or `children`; optionally a `category`, a `type`, `low` and `high`, and a ring's `max` |
| `format` | the locale's | How a value is written |
| `legend`, `toggle` | with two series, `true` | Whether a legend is written, and whether its entries are checkboxes |

The readout, the keys, the legend, the colors and the scale are the same in every form: [Chart](/components/chart) holds them, with every option, class and custom property.
