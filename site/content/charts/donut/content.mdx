A pie and a donut show what a whole is made of. Each slice is its share of the sum, kept a constant gap from its neighbors, with its corners turned. Keep a whole to five parts or fewer and fold the rest into "Other": a sixth sliver is not read.

A donut keeps its middle for one figure, the total or the part that matters, given as `center`. While a slice is under the pointer the middle says that slice's value and name instead, so nothing covers the ring. `chart-split` on the chart stands the legend beside the plot as a column, with each slice's share at its end, and puts it under the plot when there is no room.

A `pie` is the same with no hole. `--chart-size` is the width of either, 12rem by default, and `hole` is the hole's share of a donut's radius, .7 by default.

## Pie or donut \{#pie-or-donut\}

A [donut](/charts/donut) keeps its middle for one figure; a pie gives the slices the whole circle. Parts read along one bar are a [breakdown](/components/breakdown).

## Options \{#options\}

| Option | Default | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `label` | none | What the chart shows and in what unit: the plot's accessible name |
| `series` | `[]` | Each with a `name` and `values`, a `value` for a slice or a ring, or `points` for a scatter; optionally a `category`, a `type` and a ring's `max` |
| `format`, `tickFormat` | the locale's | How a value and a tick are written; `xFormat` for a scatter's scale across |
| `hole`, `center` | `.7`, none | A donut's hole, and what stands in the middle of a donut or rings |
| `legend`, `toggle` | with two series, `true` | Whether a legend is written, and whether its entries are checkboxes |

The readout, the keys, the legend, the colors and the scale are the same in every form: [Chart](/components/chart) holds them, with every option, class and custom property.
