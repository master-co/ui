A waterfall shows how one figure became another: cash at the start of a period, what came in and what went out, and cash at its end. The first value stands on the baseline, each one after it is a step from where the last left off, and a hairline carries each bar's end to the next so the eye follows the count. A step up and a step down are told apart by a status ink and by the sign written with their value.

`total` names a closing bar that stands on the baseline at the sum. Without it the chart ends on its last step, which suits a list of changes with no figure to arrive at. `values: true` writes each step over its bar with its sign; the readout adds where the count stood after it.

## Up, down and the status inks \{#inks\}

A waterfall is the one form whose marks take the status inks: a step up is drawn in the success ink and a step down in the danger ink, as a [stat](/components/stat)'s change is. The ink is never alone: the sign is written with every value, in the readout and in each point's name. Where up is not good, such as costs, say so in the chart's `label` and its title.

## Options \{#options\}

| Option | Default | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `label` | none | What the chart shows and in what unit: the plot's accessible name |
| `labels` | `[]` | The names along the scale, around a radar or over a heatmap's columns |
| `series` | `[]` | Each with a `name` and what its form reads: `values`, a `value`, `points`, `ranges` or `children`; optionally a `category`, a `type`, `low` and `high`, and a ring's `max` |
| `total` | none | A waterfall's closing bar: its name |
| `min`, `max`, `ticks` | fit, `4` | The ends of the scale and about how many steps mark it; `xMin`, `xMax` and `xTicks` for a scatter's scale across |
| `format`, `tickFormat` | the locale's | How a value and a tick are written; `xFormat` for a scatter's scale across |
| `dots`, `values` | `false` | Mark every point of a line; write every bar's value |
| `current` | none | The index of the point marked `aria-current` |

The readout, the keys, the legend, the colors and the scale are the same in every form: [Chart](/components/chart) holds them, with every option, class and custom property.
