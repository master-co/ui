## How it is built \{#how-it-is-built\}

- **A [page](/layouts/page) with one measure.** The main region is `max-w(1040) mx-auto` and a size container, `container-inline`.
- **Two columns from 56rem.** A fixed `w(200)` navigation and a fluid form, `grow(1) basis(0) min-w(0)`, in a `flex` row.
- **Two navigations, one on show.** Below 56rem of main region the [tabs](/components/tabs) are shown and the [nav](/components/nav) is `hidden`; from 56rem it is the other way round, with `hidden@container(--4xl)` on one and `hidden flex@container(--4xl)` on the other. The one that is hidden is `display: none`, so assistive technology meets only one.
- **The form looks after itself.** `form-section` puts each heading beside its fields from 40rem of form and above them below that; `form-row` and `form-actions` do the same at their own widths. The page's layout and the form's never need to agree on a breakpoint.

A long settings page can keep the navigation in view: add `sticky top(72)` to the `nav` and it follows the scroll under a 56px navbar.

## One page or many \{#one-or-many\}

Give each group its own page when saving one should not save another: a "Save changes" button at the foot of a long page is easy to miss, and a reader cannot tell which sections it covers. On one page, save each section by itself or save on change, and say which.

## Accessibility \{#accessibility\}

- Both navigations carry the same label; only one is in the accessibility tree at a time.
- Mark the current group with `aria-current="page"`.
- Each section of the form is a `<section>` named by its title.
