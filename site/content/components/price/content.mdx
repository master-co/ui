A price is what something costs, set the way a price is read: the figures large, the currency mark small and hung from their top, the period quiet on their baseline. It has no surface of its own. Put it in a plan's [card](/components/card), beside a product, or at the foot of an invoice.

## Parts \{#parts\}

- `price-amount` is the figures, in tabular numerals with a tight fit. It may be a word: Free, Custom.
- `price-currency` is the currency mark. It is under half the size of the figures and its top is level with theirs, so it reads as part of the number without competing with it.
- `price-period` says per what: a month, a seat, a thousand requests. It is small and muted on the baseline. Write it in words; "/mo" is read aloud as "slash mo".
- `price-was` is a price that no longer holds. It stands before the amount, quiet and struck through. Use an `<s>` for it, which says the text is no longer accurate.

Write the parts in reading order: was, currency, amount, period. For a currency that follows its figures, put `price-currency` after `price-amount`.

## Sizes \{#sizes\}

The figures are 36px. `price-sm` is 24px, for a row or a small card, and `price-lg` is 56px, for the one price a page is about. Every part is sized from the figures, so `--price-size` scales the whole price to any size between.

## Price or stat \{#price-or-stat\}

A price is a cost to weigh. A measurement with a label and a change, such as revenue this month, is a [stat](/components/stat). An amount that is one cell among many belongs in a [table](/components/table) with `table-numeric`.

## Accessibility \{#accessibility\}

- The parts are read in the order they are written: "dollar 29 per month". Keep the currency mark as text, never as a background image.
- A struck price is not announced as struck by every screen reader. Add a few words a reader does not see, such as "was" before it and "now" before the amount, when the difference matters.
- Do not rely on the size of the figures to say which plan is recommended: say it in the card.
