A gantt shows things that last as bars on one scale of time: the phases of a project, on-call shifts, a release train. It is a list. Each row carries where its bar begins and ends, its name, and what the bar says as text, and the stylesheet does the placing: names in one column, bars in the other, a hairline at every unit of the scale. There is no script and no SVG.

## How a bar gets its place \{#how-a-bar-gets-its-place\}

The scale runs from `--gantt-from` to `--gantt-to`, set on the list. The unit is yours: days, weeks, hours, sprints. Each `gantt-row` sets `--from` and `--to` in the same unit, and its `gantt-bar` begins and ends at that share of the track. Fractions work, so a bar can end in the middle of a unit.

The names take the width of the longest one and the track takes the rest; every row shares the two columns, so the bars line up without a width written anywhere. A hairline marks each unit of the scale behind the bars.

A bar's text is what the bar says: its dates, its length, who has it. Write it even when the bar is too short to show it, since it is what a screen reader reads; text that does not fit ends in an ellipsis.

## Progress, the current row and now \{#progress\}

`--progress` on a row, from 0 to 100, fills the bar with a firmer tint that far. The row marked `aria-current="true"` has an ink bar: the one phase a reader is in, or the one a view is about.

`gantt-now` on the list draws an ink line down every row at `--gantt-now`, for today.

## Milestones \{#milestones\}

A milestone is a point, not a span. Put `gantt-milestone` on the bar of a row that sets only `--from`: it is drawn as a small ink diamond on that point. Its text stays in the list for assistive technology and is not shown, so name the row for what happens there.

## The scale \{#the-scale\}

A row with `gantt-scale` names the units. It holds one `gantt-ticks` list with an item for each unit, and each name stands over the hairline that begins its unit. It is a picture of what the bars already say in words, so hide it with `aria-hidden`.

## In a narrow container \{#narrow\}

The gantt is a size container. Under 26rem of its own width each name stands over its bar instead of beside it, so the track keeps the whole width. Keep the scale short on a phone: eight units read, thirty do not.

## Gantt, timeline or chart \{#gantt-timeline-or-chart\}

A gantt is for things with a beginning and an end. Moments in the order they happened are a [timeline](/components/timeline). Amounts compared with each other, with no place in time, are a [chart](/components/chart).

## Accessibility \{#accessibility\}

- Write it as an `<ol>` and name it with `aria-label` or a heading. Each row is an item whose name and bar text are read in order: "Design, weeks 3 to 6".
- The bars, the hairlines and the line of now are drawn by the stylesheet. Say in the text what they show: a bar's dates, and that a row is the current one where it matters.
- Colour is not used: the current row is told apart by ink against a tint, and in forced colors by a filled bar against outlined ones.
