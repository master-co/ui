## Sheet \{#sheet\}

A sheet is a raised face with a 16px corner, drawn as a squircle where the browser can. It catches the light on its top edge, takes a half-pixel contour and settles on a short shadow that fades out. The default sheet is lifted; choose a lower or higher step by its role.

<div class="sheet sheet-raised p-lg text-sm fg-text-muted">Raised</div>
<div class="sheet p-lg text-sm fg-text-muted">Lifted</div>
<div class="sheet sheet-floating p-lg text-sm fg-text-muted">Floating</div>`} />

| Class | Elevation | Use for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `sheet sheet-flat` | One contour, no shadow | Dense lists and tables |
| `sheet sheet-raised` | `--shadow-raised` | Small cards on a busy surface |
| `sheet` | `--shadow-lifted` | Cards and panels |
| `sheet sheet-floating` | `--shadow-floating` | Popovers and menus over content |
| `sheet sheet-inverse` | The lifted shadow, on ink | The one surface on a page that asks for something |

## Inverse \{#inverse\}

`sheet-inverse` is a sheet of ink: dark on moonstone and pearl on obsidian. It is the face of a primary [button](/components/button) at the size of a surface, with the same fall of light and the same line of light along its top. Use one on a page, for the moment that asks for something, such as a [call to action](/blocks/cta).

Words on it take the inverse ink: add `fg-text-inverse` to a [heading](/components/heading), which is in strong ink by default. A default button on it is the brightest thing in view, so it reads as the action; a primary button would be ink on ink.

## Well \{#well\}

A well is recessed below its surroundings: a soft inset shadow, one half-pixel contour and a lip of light under its bottom edge. Fields, segmented tracks and switches are wells.

## Tray \{#tray\}

A tray is a recessed holder for one or more sheets. Its 4px inset makes the radii nest: the tray's corner is the sheet's corner plus that inset, 20px around 16px.

## The surface color \{#the-surface-color\}

Each surface publishes its color as `--surface-color`. Components that cut a shape out of the surface behind them read it: the ring around a [corner badge](/components/badge#on-an-icon), around [stacked avatars](/components/avatar#stack) and around a status dot. Inside a sheet, a tray, a well, a [card](/components/card) or a [dialog](/components/dialog) the ring therefore matches by itself. On a surface of your own, set the property to its background color.
