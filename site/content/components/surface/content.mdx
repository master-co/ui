## Sheet \{#sheet\}

A sheet is a raised face. It catches the light on its top edge and settles on a converged shadow. The default sheet is lifted; choose a lower or higher step by its role.

<ComponentPreview name="sheets" title="Sheet elevations" bodyClass="grid grid-cols(2) gap-lg p-lg" html={`<div class="sheet sheet-flat p-lg text-sm fg-text-muted">Flat</div>
<div class="sheet sheet-raised p-lg text-sm fg-text-muted">Raised</div>
<div class="sheet p-lg text-sm fg-text-muted">Lifted</div>
<div class="sheet sheet-floating p-lg text-sm fg-text-muted">Floating</div>`} />

| Class | Elevation | Use for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `sheet sheet-flat` | One contour, no shadow | Dense lists and tables |
| `sheet sheet-raised` | `--shadow-raised` | Small cards on a busy surface |
| `sheet` | `--shadow-lifted` | Cards and panels |
| `sheet sheet-floating` | `--shadow-floating` | Popovers and menus over content |

## Well \{#well\}

A well is recessed below its surroundings: an inset shadow, one contour and a lip of light under its bottom edge. Fields, segmented tracks and switches are wells.

<ComponentPreview name="well" title="Well" bodyClass="p-lg" html={`<div class="well p-lg text-sm fg-text-muted">Drop files here</div>`} />

## Tray \{#tray\}

A tray is a recessed holder for one or more sheets. Its 4px inset makes the radii nest: a 14px tray around a 10px sheet.
