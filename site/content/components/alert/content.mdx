An alert is a message in the flow of the page, about the page it sits in. It is a flat face with a half-pixel contour. A status tints the face faintly and colors the icon; the words stay in ink, so the hue never carries the meaning alone.

## Status \{#status\}

Choose the status by what the reader has to do, and say it in the words as well.

- `alert-info` adds context that changes nothing yet.
- `alert-success` confirms something that just completed.
- `alert-warning` asks for attention before something goes wrong.
- `alert-danger` reports a failure or a blocked action.

## Layout \{#layout\}

Put `icon alert-icon` on the icon: it centers on the first line of text, however many lines follow. `alert-body` takes the remaining width. A button or a link after the body sits at the end of the row and wraps under the text when the alert is narrow.

## Accessibility \{#accessibility\}

- An alert that appears after the page has loaded needs a live region: `role="status"` waits for a pause, and `role="alert"` interrupts. Keep `role="alert"` for failures that need action now.
- An alert that is part of the page from the start needs no role.
- Mark the icon `aria-hidden="true"`; the text carries the message.
