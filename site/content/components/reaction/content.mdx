A reaction is an emoji a reader leaves under a [message](/components/message), with how many others left it. Each one is a flat pill of the chip tone around a native checkbox: a press adds the reader's own and a second press takes it back. The count is a CSS counter, so it goes up and down with the box and needs no script.

Put a `reaction-group` after the bubble: under a received message it starts where the bubble starts, under a sent one it ends where the bubble ends. Name the group with `aria-label`, and say whose message it answers when there are several on the screen.

## The count \{#count\}

Set `--count` on each reaction to how many others gave it, not counting the reader. The pill starts its counter there, and the checked box adds one, so a reaction the reader has given shows one more than `--count`. Leave `reaction-count` empty and the counter writes the number into it; write text inside it, such as `1.2k`, when the number is too large to count by one, and that text is shown instead.

## States \{#states\}

At rest a reaction is a flat pill on one contour, and its count is muted. Under the pointer the contour is firmer; under a press the pill sinks into a well and the emoji is pushed in with it. Given, it takes the library's selected state: an ink hairline, a faint tint of ink and the count in strong ink. A disabled box, such as one in a closed thread, lies flat with its emoji faded.

## Adding a reaction \{#adding\}

`reaction-add` is a round button at the end of the row, with a face and a plus, that opens the page's emoji picker: a [popover](/components/popover) of quiet square [buttons](/components/button), each holding a `reaction-emoji`. The button stays sunk while the popover right after it is open. Choosing an emoji gives that reaction, and adds its pill when nobody has given it yet; that last step is the page's, so the picker needs a few lines of script. Everything else on this page works without one.

## Accessibility \{#accessibility\}

- Name each checkbox with the emoji and the others' count in words, such as `aria-label="Thumbs up, 3 others"`, and hide the emoji and the count with `aria-hidden="true"`. The checked state then says whether the reader is one of them, and the name stays true after every press.
- Do not rely on the drawn count being read. Engines leave text written by a CSS counter out of an accessible name and description: in Chromium the box is named by its label's words without the number.
- Name the group, and name `reaction-add` ("Add a reaction"). Each button of the picker is named with its emoji's name, and its emoji is hidden.
- The pill is the target: 28px, and 44px on a touch screen. In forced colors it keeps an outline, and a given reaction a thicker one in the highlight color.

## Platform notes \{#platform\}

The count's figures are trimmed to their cap height with `text-box: trim-both cap alphabetic`, so the pill centres the figures rather than the line around them, level with the middle of the emoji. Without it they stand within half a pixel of the middle. `text-box` is in Chromium and Safari.
