An icon frame is a small well an icon stands in: beside a heading, at the top of an empty view, in a row of features. It holds the icon at its size and center and gives it a material, so the icon reads as a mark rather than a stray glyph. It is decoration and not a control: hide it with `aria-hidden` and let the words beside it say what it means.

## Sizes \{#sizes\}

The well is 48px with a 20px icon by default. Each size takes the corner of its role, so the frame reads as the same shape at every size: 32px with the small control's corner, 40px with the control's, 48px with the large control's and 64px with the surface's.

## Faces and tones \{#faces-and-tones\}

`icon-frame-raised` makes the frame a small sheet instead of a well, for one that stands on a tray or a well. A status tone washes the well with its hue, beside words that say the same; `icon-frame-category` takes the [category](/components/category) set around it.

## Accessibility \{#accessibility\}

- The frame is decoration: `aria-hidden="true"` on it, and the meaning in the words beside it.
- It is never a button. A pressable icon is a [`btn-square`](/components/button#with-icons) with an accessible name.
- A tone's hue repeats what the words say; it never says it alone.
