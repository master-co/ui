Progress shows how much of a task is done. Use `.progress` on a native `<progress>`: the browser reports the value to assistive technology, and the class draws a thin well that fills with ink. For work of unknown length inside a control, use a [spinner](/components/spinner).

<ComponentPreview name="default" height={96} title="Progress" background="plain" bodyClass="p-lg" html={`<label class="field">
  Uploading 3 of 5 files
  <progress class="progress" value="62" max="100">62%</progress>
</label>`} />

## Indeterminate \{#indeterminate\}

Leave out `value` while the amount is unknown. A band of ink travels along the track; with reduced motion the track rests at an even half tone instead.

<ComponentPreview name="indeterminate" height={96} title="Indeterminate" background="plain" bodyClass="p-lg" html={`<label class="field">
  Preparing the build
  <progress class="progress"></progress>
</label>`} />

## Sizes \{#sizes\}

The track is 6px thick. `progress-sm` is 4px, for a row in a list, and `progress-lg` is 8px.

<ComponentPreview name="sizes" height={104} title="Sizes" background="plain" bodyClass="flex flex-col gap-md p-lg" html={`<progress class="progress progress-sm" value="30" max="100" aria-label="Small">30%</progress>
<progress class="progress" value="55" max="100" aria-label="Medium">55%</progress>
<progress class="progress progress-lg" value="80" max="100" aria-label="Large">80%</progress>`} />

## Segmented \{#segmented\}

`progress-segmented` cuts the track into equal pieces, for progress that is a count of things: three of five steps, four of eight uploads. Set `--progress-steps` to the same number as `max`, so a piece is a step. The cut is a mask over the well and its fill alike, which keeps the pieces in step with the value.

<ComponentPreview name="segmented" height={136} title="Segmented" background="plain" bodyClass="flex flex-col gap-md p-lg" html={`<label class="field">
  Step 3 of 5
  <progress class="progress progress-segmented" max="5" value="3">Step 3 of 5</progress>
</label>
<label class="field">
  5 of 8 files uploaded
  <progress class="progress progress-segmented progress-lg" style="--progress-steps: 8" max="8" value="5">5 of 8</progress>
</label>`} />

## Ring \{#ring\}

`progress-ring` draws the same amount around a circle, for a tile, a row or the corner of a card. It is an element with `role="progressbar"`. Where the browser supports typed `attr()`, the ring reads `aria-valuenow` and `aria-valuemax` itself, so the amount is written once, for people and for the drawing, and the arc eases to each new value. Elsewhere, set `--progress-ring-value` in a `style` attribute as well.

The element's own content is the label in the middle. Both ends of the arc are round, and an empty ring is an empty track.

<ComponentPreview name="ring" height={144} narrowHeight={232} title="Ring" background="plain" bodyClass="flex flex-wrap items-center justify-center gap-lg p-lg" html={`<div class="progress-ring progress-ring-sm" role="progressbar" aria-label="Upload" aria-valuenow="40" style="--progress-ring-value: 40"></div>
<div class="progress-ring" role="progressbar" aria-label="Storage used" aria-valuenow="64" style="--progress-ring-value: 64">64</div>
<div class="progress-ring progress-ring-lg" role="progressbar" aria-label="Steps done" aria-valuenow="3" aria-valuemax="8" aria-valuetext="3 of 8" style="--progress-ring-value: 3; --progress-ring-max: 8">3/8</div>
<div class="progress-ring progress-ring-xl" role="progressbar" aria-label="Coverage" aria-valuenow="86" style="--progress-ring-value: 86">86%</div>`} />

Without `aria-valuenow` the amount is unknown, and a quarter arc travels the track. Set `--progress-ring-color` to a status color when the ring reports one, and say the status in words beside it.

<ComponentPreview name="ring-states" height={112} title="Ring states" background="plain" bodyClass="flex flex-wrap items-center justify-center gap-lg p-lg" html={`<div class="progress-ring" role="progressbar" aria-label="Preparing"></div>
<div class="progress-ring progress-ring-lg" role="progressbar" aria-label="Checks passed" aria-valuenow="100" style="--progress-ring-value: 100; --progress-ring-color: var(--color-success)">100</div>
<div class="progress-ring progress-ring-lg" role="progressbar" aria-label="Quota used" aria-valuenow="92" style="--progress-ring-value: 92; --progress-ring-color: var(--color-danger)">92</div>`} />

## With a value \{#with-a-value\}

The bar does not print its number. Put it beside the label in tabular figures so it does not shift as it counts.

<ComponentPreview name="value" height={96} title="With a value" background="plain" bodyClass="p-lg" html={`<div class="flex flex-col gap-xs">
  <div class="flex items-baseline justify-between text-sm">
    <span class="fg-text-strong" id="backup-label">Uploading the backup</span>
    <span class="fg-text-muted (font-variant-numeric:tabular-nums)">7.4 of 10 GB</span>
  </div>
  <progress class="progress" value="7.4" max="10" aria-labelledby="backup-label">74%</progress>
</div>`} />

## A marker over the end \{#marker\}

`progress-label` holds the bar and a `progress-marker` that rides the end of its fill: the figure on a small sheet whose point touches the end. The sheet slides under its point as far as the point is along the bar, so at 0% and at 100% it stays inside the bar's ends and never leaves the container. Room is kept above the bar for it, or below it with `progress-label-below`.

<ComponentPreview name="marker" height={176} title="A marker over the end" background="plain" bodyClass="flex flex-col justify-center gap-xl p-lg" html={`<div class="progress-label" style="--progress-value: 62">
  <progress class="progress" value="62" max="100" aria-label="Uploading the archive">62%</progress>
  <span class="progress-marker" aria-hidden="true">62%</span>
</div>
<div class="progress-label progress-label-below" style="--progress-value: 96">
  <progress class="progress" value="96" max="100" aria-label="Indexing">96%</progress>
  <span class="progress-marker" aria-hidden="true">96%</span>
</div>`} />

The bar is the semantics and the marker is `aria-hidden`; set `--progress-value` to the bar's amount as a percentage, written beside its `value`, as a renderer writes both at once. An indeterminate bar has no amount, so its marker is not shown. The marker eases along with the fill, and with reduced motion it jumps.

## Half ring \{#half-ring\}

`progress-ring-half` draws the amount along an arch, the top half of the ring, with the figure standing in it: a score, a quota, the share of a goal. Both ends of the arch and the end of the arc are round. Without `aria-valuenow` the amount is unknown and a quarter of the arch travels from one end to the other.

<ComponentPreview name="half-ring" height={128} narrowHeight={200} title="Half ring" background="plain" bodyClass="flex flex-wrap items-end justify-center gap-xl p-lg" html={`<div class="progress-ring progress-ring-half progress-ring-xl" role="progressbar" aria-label="Storage used" aria-valuenow="72" style="--progress-ring-value: 72">72%</div>
<div class="progress-ring progress-ring-half progress-ring-lg" role="progressbar" aria-label="Seats taken" aria-valuenow="18" aria-valuemax="40" style="--progress-ring-value: 18; --progress-ring-max: 40">18</div>
<div class="progress-ring progress-ring-half progress-ring-lg" role="progressbar" aria-label="Estimating"></div>`} />

## Accessibility \{#accessibility\}

- Label the bar with a `field` label, `aria-label` or `aria-labelledby`.
- The text inside `<progress>` is a fallback for browsers that do not draw the element.
- When the bar describes a region that is loading, set `aria-busy="true"` on that region until it is done.
