A color picker chooses any color: lightness and chroma on a field under one thumb, the hue and the opacity on two [sliders](/components/slider), and the color itself as text in hex, `rgb()` or `oklch()`. It chooses in OKLCH, the space every color of the theme is written in, so a step of lightness looks like the same step at every hue. The field is a picture set into the surface; the thumb is the color in a pearl ring; the tracks are native range inputs. The `color-picker` [behavior](/guide/behaviors) keeps the parts in step.

## Opened from a button \{#popover\}

Most of the time a color is one setting among others, and the picker opens from a button that shows it. Put the button and a [popover](/components/popover) with `color-picker-popover` inside the root: the popover becomes the column, and a swatch with `color-picker-swatch` in the button shows the color while the picker is closed, since both stand in the root that holds it. The browser opens, places and dismisses the popover; the behavior is the same.

Leave out `color-picker-alpha` for a color that has to be opaque, such as a brand color that is printed: the swatch then stands beside the hue alone, and a color typed with an opacity is taken without it. Leave out the swatch too, and the tracks take the whole width.

## Why OKLCH \{#oklch\}

The field could be saturation and value, the square most pickers draw, which is easy to paint because it is only sRGB mixed with white and black. It is a poor field to choose a palette on: the same value is a dark blue and a glaring yellow, and a hue moved at the same saturation changes how light the color looks. OKLCH is built so that equal steps look equal, which is why the theme writes its colors in it, and why a picker for a theme should choose in it too.

- **Lightness** runs from 100% at the top of the field to 0 at the bottom, and is the same lightness at every hue.
- **Chroma** runs from none, a grey, at the start of the field to .37 at its end, as far as Display P3 reaches. A color is not always as vivid as its chroma asks: the screen shows the nearest one it can, and so do the field and the thumb, through the same rule.
- **Hue** runs round the circle on its own slider, painted at one lightness and chroma so every hue can be told apart.

The value is written in the format the select says. `oklch()` is written exactly as it is held. Hex and `rgb()` can hold only sRGB, so a color past it is fitted as CSS Color 4 fits a color to a screen: chroma is taken away, at the same lightness and hue, until the color is within a just noticeable difference of one sRGB can write. Switching the format never moves the color held, so a color goes from hex to `oklch()` and back to the same hex.

A gradient runs along one axis only, so the field is drawn in rows: each row is a gradient from grey to .37 at its own lightness, exact along the line, and the rows halfway between them lie over the others under a mask that fades from one row to the next. The colors are the theme's mixins, functions of the hue the behavior publishes, so the component writes no color of its own.

## The behavior \{#behavior\}

`colorPicker` takes the root, the element with the `color-picker` class, and returns the function that detaches it.

```js name=app.js
import { colorPicker } from '@master/ui/behaviors/color-picker.js'

const accent = colorPicker(document.querySelector('.color-picker'))

accent.value // 'oklch(62% 0.19 260)'
accent.value = '#e5484d' // choose a color, and hex as its format
```

- **The color** starts from the text field's value, in any of the three formats, and the format with it. The behavior writes it on the root as four numbers, `--color-picker-lightness`, `--color-picker-chroma`, `--color-picker-hue` and `--color-picker-alpha`, which place the thumb and paint the field, the tracks and the swatches. Write the first color's numbers in the root's `style` too, so the field is right before the script runs.
- **The field.** A press puts the thumb under the pointer and gives it focus; a drag follows, even past the edge of the field, where the color stops. The keys are in the table below.
- **The text** takes a color as soon as what is typed is one, and leaves the text as it is typed. When the field is committed, with <kbd class="kbd">Enter</kbd> or by leaving it, the color is written again in the format chosen; text that is not a color is put back.
- **Events.** The thumb dispatches `input` while the color moves and `change` when a drag ends or a key moved it; the sliders, the select and the text field dispatch their own. Whoever listens on the root reads the new `value` in each of them.
- **The thumb's words.** Its `aria-valuetext` says `Lightness 62%, chroma 0.19`; pass `valueText: (lightness, chroma) => …` to say it in the page's language.

Without the behavior the field shows the color written in the markup, and the sliders, the select and the text field are plain controls that do not follow one another.

To place the picker outside its form, give all its native fields the same `form` attribute. Resetting that form restores the initial color and format throughout the picker. Canceling the reset preserves the current color. A disabled fieldset also disables the color area, except inside its first legend.

## Accessibility \{#accessibility\}

- The field's thumb is one slider. Its value is the lightness, since <kbd class="kbd">ArrowUp</kbd> and <kbd class="kbd">ArrowDown</kbd> move it, and its `aria-valuetext` names the lightness and the chroma together, so a screen reader says both whichever moved. Name it for what it chooses, such as "Lightness and chroma".
- The text field is the way to give an exact color without the field, and the name of the color goes on it: "Brand color". Its `name` is what a form submits.
- Name the hue and the opacity sliders and the format select; their keys are the platform's.
- Forced colors keep the field's colors, since they are what the reader came for; its edge and the thumb's focus are drawn in system colors.

## React, Svelte and Vue \{#frameworks\}

The `color-picker` component draws the first value into the first render, the four numbers on the root included, and attaches the behavior, which moves every part after that. Its value is the color as text, in the format chosen, and it comes out on every change; give `trigger` (a snippet in Svelte, a slot in Vue) to open the picker from a button of the page's. Copy it into the project with `npx master-ui add color-picker`; [React, Svelte and Vue](/guide/frameworks) says how the components are built and used.
