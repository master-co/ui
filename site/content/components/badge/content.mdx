A badge is a small chip for a status, a category or a count. Neutral badges carry most labels; hue appears when the badge states a status, beside words that say the same thing, or names a [category](/components/category) of data.

## Category \{#category\}

`badge-category` takes the surface, the text and the line of the category set by a `category-<name>` class on the badge or around it, and its dot takes the category's mark. A category says what something belongs to; a status says how it is. A badge is one or the other: `badge-category` and the four tones never share one.

## Live \{#live\}

`badge-live` makes the dot send out a ring, for something that is happening now: a recording, a deployment in progress, a stream. Use it for one thing on a view; a page of pulsing dots says nothing. With reduced motion it is a plain dot.

## Pictures and long labels \{#pictures-and-long-labels\}

`badge-media` holds a small picture at the start, an [avatar](/components/avatar) or an [icon](/components/icon), sized to the badge less two insets and kept round, so it sits in the badge's end with even room around it. Wrap a label that may be long in `badge-label`: the badge stops at its container's edge and the label ends in an ellipsis, while the picture keeps its size.

## In a title \{#in-a-title\}

A badge that qualifies a title goes inside the heading, after the words and a space. It is one inline box, so it never splits: when the last line has no room for it, the whole badge moves to the next line. It is also part of the heading's text, so a list of headings reads "Edge functions in every region Beta" and the status is not lost.

<ComponentPreview name="title" height={136} narrowHeight={168} title="In a title" background="plain" bodyClass="p-lg" html={`<div class="flex flex-col gap-xs max-w(300) mx-auto">
  <h3 class="heading">Edge functions in every region <span class="badge vertical-middle">Beta</span></h3>
  <p class="m(0) text-sm fg-text-muted">Run code within 50 ms of every visitor.</p>
</div>`} />

A badge keeps its own size inside any heading. Add `vertical-middle` so it centers on the line it lands on.

## Compound \{#compound\}

`badge-compound` holds a smaller badge, `badge-addon`, and a short message in one capsule: a version and what is new in it, a label and a date. The capsule is taller by two insets and its corner is the addon's plus the inset, so the two curves run side by side. The addon stands at the start or the end. As a link the whole capsule is one target: it takes the hover and the focus ring, and nothing inside it is a control of its own.

## On an icon \{#on-an-icon\}

`badge-corner` places a count on the corner of its nearest positioned parent and cuts it out with a ring in the color of the surface it sits on, which it reads from [`--surface-color`](/components/surface#the-surface-color). Set `--badge-ring` on a background of your own. Describe the count in the parent's accessible name.
