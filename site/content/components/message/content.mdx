A message is one turn of a conversation. A received message is a raised sheet at the start of the row; a sent one is ink at the end, the way a primary [button](/components/button) is ink. A bubble has a surface's corners, except the one that points at its author, which is tight.

The thread is any list. Use an `<ol>`, since the order is the content, and clear its margin and padding with `m(0) p(0)`; the turns bring their own rhythm. A turn stands 12px from the one before it and 4px when both come from the same side, so a change of author reads as a break. A bubble takes most of a narrow thread and never more than a comfortable measure of a wide one.

## Without avatars \{#without-avatars\}

In a conversation between two people the side already says who is speaking. Leave the avatar out and the bubble starts the row.

## Typing \{#typing\}

`message-typing` is three dots that rise in turn, inside a bubble. The dots mean nothing to assistive technology, so the bubble carries the words: `role="status"` and a label such as "Ada is typing".

## Accessibility \{#accessibility\}

- Use an `<ol>` for the thread. Put new turns in an `aria-live="polite"` region, or make the list one with `role="log"`, so arriving messages are announced without stealing focus.
- Color and side are not the only signals of authorship for a screen reader: name the author in each turn, visibly in `message-meta` or in a visually hidden prefix.
- The avatar's `alt` is the author's name when no name is written in the turn; when one is, leave it empty.
- With reduced motion the typing dots rest. A sent bubble is ink on moonstone and pearl on obsidian, at full text contrast in both.

## Classes \{#classes\}

| Class | Description |
| --- | --- |
| `message` | A received turn, at the start of the row |
| `message-sent` | A turn of your own, in ink at the end of the row |
| `message-avatar` | The author's avatar, level with the foot of the bubble |
| `message-bubble` | The words |
| `message-meta` | A name, a time, a receipt under the bubble |
| `message-typing` | Three rising dots, inside a bubble |

## Custom properties \{#custom-properties\}

| Property | Default | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `--message-radius` | `1.125rem` | A bubble's corner |
| `--message-tail` | `.375rem` | The corner that points at the author |
