A device is the body of a phone around a screenshot, a video or a running demo. It is drawn in one material, like a model cast in clay: a lifted sheet for the body, a narrow bezel, and a small well at the top of the screen where the camera would be. The frame says "this is a phone" and leaves every color to what it shows, as a [window](/components/window) does for a desktop.

## The screen \{#the-screen\}

`device-screen` goes on whatever the phone shows: an `<img>`, a `<video>`, an `<iframe>` or a block of markup. It fills the body inside the bezel, and an image is cropped to it. Its corner is the body's corner less the bezel, so the gap between the two keeps one width around the bend.

A screen made of markup starts under the camera's well: leave about a sixth of the body's width clear at the top, as a real status bar does.

## Size \{#size\}

The body is 280px wide and 9 by 19.5. Resize it with `--device-width`, not with a width: the corner is 14% of that property and the bezel 3%, so both follow and the phone keeps its shape at any size. `--device-ratio` changes the proportions, for an older phone or a small tablet.

## Accessibility \{#accessibility\}

- The frame is decoration and is not announced. The meaning is in the screen: give an image an `alt` that says what the interface shows, or an empty `alt` when the text beside it already does.
- A live demo inside the screen is a real, small page. Keep its targets at touch size, and make sure it can be operated with the keyboard or is hidden from it.
- In forced colors the body and the camera's well take a system outline.
