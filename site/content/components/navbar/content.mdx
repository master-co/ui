A navbar is the bar at the top of a page or an app: the brand, the main links and a few actions. At the top of the page it has no material at all, so the page starts as one surface. Once content scrolls under it, the bar becomes a pane of frosted glass with a hairline and a short shadow. A scroll-state container query knows when the sticky bar is stuck; no script watches the scroll.

The bar is `position: sticky` at the top of its scroll container, which is the page in most layouts. Where the browser has no scroll-state queries, the pane is always drawn.

The navbar is the bar itself: its material, its stuck state and the width at which its links step aside. What goes around it is not its business. For a whole site header, with a menu, a second action and a drawer, copy the [header](/blocks/header) block; to place the bar in a page with a sidebar, see the [app shell](/layouts/app-shell) layout.

## Narrow bars \{#narrow\}

The bar is also a size container. Below 36rem of bar, `navbar-nav` steps aside and `navbar-toggle` appears; at 36rem and above it is the other way round. Point the toggle at a [drawer](/components/dialog#drawer) or a [menu](/components/menu) that holds the same links.

## Reading progress \{#progress\}

Put a `navbar-progress` inside the bar and a hairline of ink along its foot fills as the page scrolls. It is driven by a scroll timeline on the root scroller, so it shows the progress of the page, and it is drawn only where the browser supports scroll-driven animations.

```html
<header class="navbar">
  …
  <div class="navbar-progress" role="presentation"></div>
</header>
```

## Accessibility \{#accessibility\}

- Use a `<header>` for the bar and a `<nav>` with a label for the links. Mark the current page with `aria-current="page"`: it is the only thing that puts a link in ink.
- The toggle is an ordinary button. Opened by `popovertarget` or `command`, its menu or drawer needs no script.
- The pane is decoration. With `prefers-reduced-transparency` it is opaque, and in forced colors it is the canvas with a line under it.
- A sticky bar covers the top of the page. Set `scroll-padding-top` on the scroller to the bar's height, so a link to a heading does not land under it.
