A gauge is a reading on a dial: two thirds of a ring, open at the foot, that fills with ink as far as the value has come. The figure stands in the middle and one word under it, between the two ends of the arc. It is an element with `role="meter"`, so the reading is written for people and the stylesheet draws it. There is no script and no SVG.

## How the arc gets its length \{#how-the-arc-gets-its-length\}

The reading is `aria-valuenow`, between `aria-valuemin` and `aria-valuemax`, or 0 and 100 when they are left out. Where the browser supports typed `attr()`, the gauge reads those attributes itself, so the number is written once. Elsewhere it reads `--gauge-value`, and `--gauge-min` and `--gauge-max` for another range: until typed `attr()` is in every engine, write both, as the examples here do.

The track and the fill each end in a round cap, and the fill eases to a new reading. An empty gauge is an empty track: the caps have no size at nothing.

## Sizes \{#sizes\}

A gauge is 120px across. `gauge-sm` is 88px, for a tile in a dense dashboard, and `gauge-lg` is 160px, for the one reading a view is about. The stroke is a fifteenth of the width and the figure scales with it. Without a `gauge-label`, the figure stands at the middle of the dial.

## A reading that is a status \{#status\}

The fill is ink. When the reading itself is a warning, set `--gauge-color` to a status color and say so in words: a hue never carries the meaning alone. The track is the same color stepped back, so it follows.

## Gauge, meter or progress \{#gauge-meter-or-progress\}

A gauge and a [meter](/components/meter) both show a measurement within a range. Choose the gauge when the figure is the point and has room of its own, as on a dashboard; choose the meter for a line in a form or a row of a table. [Progress](/components/progress) is for a task that completes, and its ring closes; a gauge's arc never does.

## Accessibility \{#accessibility\}

- Give the element `role="meter"`, an accessible name with `aria-label` or `aria-labelledby`, and the reading in `aria-valuenow`. Add `aria-valuemin` and `aria-valuemax` when the range is not 0 to 100.
- Write the reading again as the text of `gauge-value`, with its unit. When the number needs words to be understood, add `aria-valuetext`.
- The arc is drawn by the stylesheet and is not announced. In forced colors the fill takes the system highlight and the track the system's grey.
