A banner is one line across the top of a page or a panel, for something everyone on it should know: a release, planned maintenance, an incident. It is a band of the tray tone with a hairline under it, quieter than an [alert](/components/alert), which belongs to one task, and longer-lived than a [toast](/components/toast), which reports one event.

## Dismissing \{#dismissing\}

A banner that can be dismissed is a `<dialog open>` with `banner-dismissible`. A non-modal dialog opened by its attribute is part of the page from the first paint, and the button inside a `<form method="dialog">` closes it without script. The band then folds away to nothing, where the browser can interpolate to an automatic height; elsewhere it leaves at once.

To remember the dismissal, listen for the dialog's `close` event and store it; leave the `open` attribute out when rendering the page next time.

A banner that stays is any element, such as a `<div role="region">` with a label.

## Inverse and brand \{#inverse-and-brand\}

`banner-inverse` is a band of ink, for the one announcement of a release. `banner-brand` wears the [brand color](/guide/theme#status-and-brand), which the system keeps for one moment per view: with a brand banner on the page, no other brand surface belongs on it.

## Accessibility \{#accessibility\}

- Give a banner a role that matches its urgency: `role="status"` for news that can wait, `role="alert"` for an incident, a labelled `role="region"` or the dialog's own role for an announcement. A banner present at load is not announced; only one added later is.
- Write one sentence and at most one link. A banner that wraps to three lines on a phone has become a paragraph; move it into the page.
- The dismiss button has a name. Its target reaches the band's edge.
- Text keeps its contrast on every tone: status tones tint the band by a few percent and leave the words in ink.

## Classes \{#classes\}

| Class | Description |
| --- | --- |
| `banner` | The band |
| `banner-dismissible` | On a `<dialog open>`: folds away when closed |
| `banner-info`, `banner-success`, `banner-warning`, `banner-danger` | A status tint and icon color |
| `banner-inverse`, `banner-brand` | A band of ink, or of the brand color |
| `banner-content` | The message, centered and wrapping as a group |
| `banner-title` | The few words that carry the news |
| `banner-action` | The one link out |
| `banner-close` | The dismiss form or button, at the far end |

## Custom properties \{#custom-properties\}

| Property | Default | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `--banner-tone` | `var(--color-text-strong)` | The icon color, and the hue of a tint |
| `--banner-color` | `var(--color-surface-tray)` | The band's color |
