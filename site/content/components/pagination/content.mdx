Pagination is the pages of a long list, as a row of links inside a `<nav>`. The links are quiet square [buttons](/components/button); the current page has `aria-current="page"` and stays sunk into a well, like a pressed toggle. This stylesheet supplies the row and the gap that stands for skipped pages.

## Previous and next only \{#previous-and-next-only\}

When the number of pages is unknown or does not matter, two labelled buttons are enough. A link cannot be disabled: on the first page, give "Previous" `aria-disabled="true"` and remove its `href`.

<ComponentPreview name="simple" title="Previous and next only" background="plain" html={`<nav class="flex items-center gap-xs" aria-label="Pagination">
  <a class="btn btn-sm" aria-disabled="true">Previous</a>
  <span class="text-sm fg-text-muted px-xs">Page 1 of 24</span>
  <a class="btn btn-sm" href="#page-2">Next</a>
</nav>`} />

## Accessibility \{#accessibility\}

- Wrap the list in `<nav aria-label="Pagination">` and mark the current page with `aria-current="page"`.
- A bare number is a poor link name. Give each page link an `aria-label` such as "Page 3".
- Hide the gap with `aria-hidden="true"`: it is not a control.
