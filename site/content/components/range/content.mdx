A range picks two values on one scale: the lowest and the highest price, the start and the end of a clip. It is two native [sliders](/components/slider) in one group. The group draws a single track and fills it with ink between the thumbs; each input keeps its own keys, its own label and its own value in the form.

## No script for the fill \{#no-script-for-the-fill\}

Each thumb is a view timeline inside its own input, the way a single slider reads its fill. The group takes both timelines with `timeline-scope`, runs one animation on each and so knows two percentages. It paints from the lesser to the greater, which means the thumbs may cross: the fill is still between them, and the lower of the two values is still the start of the range.

Read the pair the same way in your form handler: the smaller value is the lower bound. If the two must keep a distance, clamp them on `input`; nothing in the drawing depends on it.

Where the browser has no scroll-driven animations the track is a plain well under the two thumbs, and both inputs work as they always have. Chromium and Safari 26 draw the fill.

## How the thumbs share a track \{#how-the-thumbs-share-a-track\}

The two inputs lie in the same grid cell. Each gives up its own track and takes the pointer only on its thumb, so a press between the thumbs reaches neither and a thumb is never hidden behind the other input's empty track. The one being moved comes to the front. A press on the bare track does nothing: with two thumbs it could not say which one it meant.

## Accessibility \{#accessibility\}

- Give the group `role="group"` and a name, and give each input its own `aria-label`: "Lowest price" and "Highest price", not "Start" and "End".
- Print the two values near the track. A thumb does not show its number.
- Each thumb is a tab stop with the keys of a native slider. On touch screens a thumb is 24px.
- When the exact numbers matter more than the feel of the scale, offer two [steppers](/components/stepper) or two number fields as well.

## Classes \{#classes\}

| Class | Description |
| --- | --- |
| `range` | The group: one track, filled between its two thumbs |
| `range-start`, `range-end` | On the two `slider` inputs: names each thumb for the fill |
