An empty state is what a view shows when it has nothing to show yet. It says what belongs here and offers the action that fills it. It is a centered column headed by an [icon frame](/components/icon-frame), and has no surface of its own: put it in a [card](/components/card), a [well](/components/surface#well) or straight on the page.

## After a search \{#after-a-search\}

When a filter or a search returns nothing, say what was searched and offer the way back. The media and the actions are optional.

## The icon \{#the-icon\}

The icon stands in an [icon frame](/components/icon-frame), which gives it its material: a well pressed into the page, or a raised sheet, `icon-frame-raised`, when the empty view stands in a well as above. `empty-media` on the frame gives it its place in the column, with a little more room under it than between the words. The frame is decoration: the title says what is missing.

## Empty search and a first collection \{#recording-list\}

Search for an absent title in the read-only and full workspaces. Clear the search or inspect usage. Switch to First visit to add a sample recording. Reload shows the same rows while loading. Removed recordings can be restored together.

## Writing one \{#writing-one\}

- The title names the state in plain words: "No deployments yet", not "Nothing here".
- The description says how the view fills, in one or two sentences. It wraps at 24rem.
- Offer one main action. A second, quieter one may lead to documentation.
