An empty state is what a view shows when it has nothing to show yet. It says what belongs here and offers the action that fills it. It is a centered column with a small well for an icon, and has no surface of its own: put it in a [card](/components/card), a [well](/components/surface#well) or straight on the page.

</div>`} />

## After a search \{#after-a-search\}

When a filter or a search returns nothing, say what was searched and offer the way back. The media and the actions are optional.

<ComponentPreview name="search" title="After a search" background="plain" bodyClass="p-lg" html={`<div class="empty">
  <h3 class="empty-title">No projects match "atlsa"</h3>
  <p class="empty-description">Check the spelling, or clear the search to see all 12 projects.</p>
  <div class="empty-actions">
    <button type="button" class="btn btn-sm">Clear search</button>
  </div>
</div>`} />

## Writing one \{#writing-one\}

- The title names the state in plain words: "No deployments yet", not "Nothing here".
- The description says how the view fills, in one or two sentences. It wraps at 24rem.
- Offer one main action. A second, quieter one may lead to documentation.
