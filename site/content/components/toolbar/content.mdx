A toolbar is a small raised sheet that holds a row of quiet controls. Its 4px inset makes the corners nest: the toolbar's corner is a small control's corner plus the inset, so small quiet [buttons](/components/button), [selects](/components/select) and [segmented controls](/components/segmented) sit in it concentrically. It is 40px tall around 32px controls.

## State in a toolbar \{#state-in-a-toolbar\}

A quiet button has no material until touched, so the toolbar's own sheet shows through. Under the pointer the button rises as a sheet of its own; pressed, or with `aria-pressed="true"`, it sinks into a well. The toolbar is where the [state order](/guide/theme#principles) is easiest to see: well, sheet, raised.

## Floating \{#floating\}

`toolbar-floating` lifts the toolbar onto the floating surface, for tools that sit over content: a selection bar, or the controls of an editor.

## Accessibility \{#accessibility\}

- Give the container `role="toolbar"` and a label.
- The toolbar pattern moves between controls with the arrow keys and keeps one tab stop. That needs a small script of the page's own; without it every control is a tab stop, which works and is only slower.
- Name icon-only buttons with `aria-label`, and say toggles with `aria-pressed`.
