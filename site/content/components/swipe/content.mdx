A swipe is a row that slides aside to show what can be done to it. The row is a scroller with two places to rest: its content, as wide as the row, and the actions that wait behind its end edge. The browser's own scrolling does the work, so a finger, a trackpad and the <kbd>Tab</kbd> key all bring the actions in, and there is no script.

## How it works \{#how-it-works\}

`swipe` is a horizontal scroller with no scrollbar and mandatory scroll snapping. `swipe-content` is as wide as the row and snaps at its start; `swipe-actions` follows it and snaps at its end. Letting go between the two settles on the nearer one, and scrolling back closes the row.

The content is opaque: it takes the color of the surface it lies on from `--surface-color`, which a [sheet](/components/surface) and a card publish, and casts a short shade on the well it uncovers. Where scroll-driven animations are supported, the actions fade in as they are uncovered; elsewhere they are simply there.

Nothing closes a row when another opens, and nothing removes a row when its action is pressed: both are the page's business. To close a row from script, set its `scrollLeft` to 0.

## Actions \{#actions\}

An action is a button or a link: a word, or an [icon](/components/icon) over a word. Each cell is at least 72px wide and as tall as the row, so it is a full touch target at any row height. `swipe-action-danger` is the action that cannot be undone, in the danger ink on its own tint. Keep to one or two actions, with the destructive one last.

## Accessibility \{#accessibility\}

- **Swiping is a shortcut, never the only way.** A reader with a mouse and no trackpad cannot scroll a row sideways. Offer the same actions somewhere a pointer finds them: a [menu](/components/menu) on the row, or on the opened item.
- The actions are real buttons in the tab order. When one takes focus, the browser scrolls it into view, so the keyboard needs nothing else. The focus ring is drawn inside the cell, where the scroller does not cut it.
- An action that shows only an icon needs an accessible name. A word under the icon is better: these targets are found by reading.
- Confirm a destructive action, or offer to undo it in a [toast](/components/toast).
