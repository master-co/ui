A checkbox is a small well that fills with ink when checked. Put `.checkbox` on a native `<input type="checkbox">`, and wrap it with its text in a `<label class="check">` so the whole row is a target. Each checkbox is its own yes or no; for one choice among several, use a [radio](/components/radio).

<ComponentPreview name="default" height={104} title="Checkbox" background="plain" bodyClass="flex flex-col items-start gap-sm p-lg" html={`<label class="check"><input type="checkbox" class="checkbox" checked> Email me about releases</label>
<label class="check"><input type="checkbox" class="checkbox"> Weekly digest</label>`} />

## In a group \{#in-a-group\}

Related checkboxes go in a [`fieldset`](/components/input#fieldset) with a legend, so the group has a name. Any number of them can be checked.

<ComponentPreview name="group" height={168} title="In a group" background="plain" bodyClass="p-lg" html={`<fieldset class="fieldset">
  <legend class="fieldset-legend">Notify me about</legend>
  <label class="check"><input type="checkbox" class="checkbox" name="notify" value="failed" checked> Failed deployments</label>
  <label class="check"><input type="checkbox" class="checkbox" name="notify" value="certificates" checked> Expiring certificates</label>
  <label class="check"><input type="checkbox" class="checkbox" name="notify" value="news"> Product news</label>
</fieldset>`} />

## Long labels \{#long-labels\}

The box centers on the first line of its label and stays there when the label wraps, so a sentence can follow it.

<ComponentPreview name="long" height={112} narrowHeight={136} title="Long labels" background="plain" bodyClass="p-lg" html={`<label class="check max-w(320)">
  <input type="checkbox" class="checkbox" required>
  I have read the terms of service and understand that deployments on the free plan pause after 30 days without traffic.
</label>`} />

When an option needs a title and a description of its own, use a [choice](/components/choice).

## A link beside the label \{#a-link-beside-the-label\}

The whole label is the checkbox's target, so a link inside it makes two targets of one line: a press on the link's words follows the link, and a press a few pixels away checks the box. Keep the link out of the label, as the next thing in the row. Each then has its own target and its own stop in the tab order, and reading the terms leaves the box as it was.

<ComponentPreview name="link" height={96} narrowHeight={128} title="A link beside the label" background="plain" bodyClass="p-lg" html={`<div class="flex flex-wrap items-center gap-xs max-w(360) mx-auto text-sm">
  <label class="check"><input type="checkbox" class="checkbox" name="terms" required> I agree to the terms of service</label>
  <a class="link" href="#terms">Read the terms</a>
</div>`} />

## Indeterminate \{#indeterminate\}

A parent checkbox whose children are partly selected shows a dash. Set `indeterminate` from script; HTML has no attribute for it.

<ComponentPreview name="indeterminate" height={136} title="Indeterminate" background="plain" bodyClass="flex flex-col items-start gap-sm p-lg" html={`<label class="check"><input type="checkbox" class="checkbox" id="select-all"> All regions</label>
<label class="check ml-lg"><input type="checkbox" class="checkbox" checked> Tokyo</label>
<label class="check ml-lg"><input type="checkbox" class="checkbox"> Frankfurt</label>
<script>document.getElementById('select-all').indeterminate = true</script>`} />


## Required \{#required\}

A `required` box left empty takes the danger edge of a [field](/components/input#native-validation) once the reader has tried to send the form, through `:user-invalid`, and keeps it under the pointer; `aria-invalid="true"` draws the same from a script. The words that say why are the browser's own message, or a `field-error` beside the box.

<ComponentPreview name="required" height={136} title="Required" background="plain" bodyClass="p-lg" html={`<form class="flex flex-col items-start gap-md">
  <label class="check"><input type="checkbox" class="checkbox" name="terms" required> I agree to the terms of service</label>
  <button type="submit" class="btn btn-primary">Create account</button>
</form>
<script>
  document.querySelector('form').addEventListener('submit', event => event.preventDefault())
</script>`} caption="Press Create account with the box empty." />

## Disabled \{#disabled\}

A disabled checkbox fades, and its label drops to disabled ink.

<ComponentPreview name="disabled" height={104} title="Disabled" background="plain" bodyClass="flex flex-col items-start gap-sm p-lg" html={`<label class="check"><input type="checkbox" class="checkbox" checked disabled> Required by your plan</label>
<label class="check"><input type="checkbox" class="checkbox" disabled> Not available in your region</label>`} />

## Shape \{#shape\}

A checkbox is a square with a chip's corner, which says "any of these". Where the browser draws [continuous corners](/guide/theme#continuous-corners), that square takes the shape of an app icon. The check arrives with a short settle and leaves at once; with reduced motion it only fades.

## Accessibility \{#accessibility\}

- The input is native, so <kbd>Space</kbd> toggles it and a form submits it.
- A checkbox without visible text, such as one in a [table](/components/table#selection) row, needs `aria-label`.
- A setting that takes effect at once, without a save button, is a [switch](/components/switch).
