A checkbox is a small well that fills with ink when checked. Put `.checkbox` on a native `<input type="checkbox">`, and wrap it with its text in a `<label class="check">` so the whole row is a target. Each checkbox is its own yes or no; for one choice among several, use a [radio](/components/radio).

<ComponentPreview name="default" title="Checkbox" background="plain" bodyClass="flex flex-col items-start gap-sm p-lg" html={`<label class="check"><input type="checkbox" class="checkbox" checked> Email me about releases</label>
<label class="check"><input type="checkbox" class="checkbox"> Weekly digest</label>`} />

## In a group \{#in-a-group\}

Related checkboxes go in a [`fieldset`](/components/input#fieldset) with a legend, so the group has a name. Any number of them can be checked.

<ComponentPreview name="group" title="In a group" background="plain" bodyClass="p-lg" html={`<fieldset class="fieldset">
  <legend class="fieldset-legend">Notify me about</legend>
  <label class="check"><input type="checkbox" class="checkbox" name="notify" value="failed" checked> Failed deployments</label>
  <label class="check"><input type="checkbox" class="checkbox" name="notify" value="certificates" checked> Expiring certificates</label>
  <label class="check"><input type="checkbox" class="checkbox" name="notify" value="news"> Product news</label>
</fieldset>`} />

## Long labels \{#long-labels\}

The box centers on the first line of its label and stays there when the label wraps, so a sentence can follow it.

<ComponentPreview name="long" title="Long labels" background="plain" bodyClass="p-lg" html={`<label class="check max-w(320)">
  <input type="checkbox" class="checkbox" required>
  I have read the terms of service and understand that deployments on the free plan pause after 30 days without traffic.
</label>`} />

When an option needs a title and a description of its own, use a [choice](/components/choice).

## Indeterminate \{#indeterminate\}

A parent checkbox whose children are partly selected shows a dash. Set `indeterminate` from script; HTML has no attribute for it.

<ComponentPreview name="indeterminate" title="Indeterminate" background="plain" bodyClass="flex flex-col items-start gap-sm p-lg" html={`<label class="check"><input type="checkbox" class="checkbox" id="select-all"> All regions</label>
<label class="check ml-lg"><input type="checkbox" class="checkbox" checked> Tokyo</label>
<label class="check ml-lg"><input type="checkbox" class="checkbox"> Frankfurt</label>
<script>document.getElementById('select-all').indeterminate = true</script>`} />

## Disabled \{#disabled\}

A disabled checkbox fades, and its label drops to disabled ink.

<ComponentPreview name="disabled" title="Disabled" background="plain" bodyClass="flex flex-col items-start gap-sm p-lg" html={`<label class="check"><input type="checkbox" class="checkbox" checked disabled> Required by your plan</label>
<label class="check"><input type="checkbox" class="checkbox" disabled> Not available in your region</label>`} />

## Shape \{#shape\}

A checkbox is a square with a chip's corner, which says "any of these". Where the browser draws [continuous corners](/guide/theme#continuous-corners), that square takes the shape of an app icon. The check arrives with a short settle and leaves at once; with reduced motion it only fades.

## Accessibility \{#accessibility\}

- The input is native, so <kbd>Space</kbd> toggles it and a form submits it.
- A checkbox without visible text, such as one in a [table](/components/table#selection) row, needs `aria-label`.
- A setting that takes effect at once, without a save button, is a [switch](/components/switch).
