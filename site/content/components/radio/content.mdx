A radio is a small round well that fills with ink and shows a dot when chosen. A circle says "one of these", where a [checkbox](/components/checkbox)'s square says "any of these". Put `.radio` on a native `<input type="radio">`, and wrap it with its text in a `<label class="check">` so the whole row is a target.

  <label class="check"><input type="radio" class="radio" name="visibility" value="team"> Team only</label>

</fieldset>`} />

## A group \{#a-group\}

Radios that share a `name` form one group: choosing one clears the others, and the arrow keys move the choice along the group. Put the group in a [`fieldset`](/components/input#fieldset) with a legend, which names the question the options answer.

## In a row \{#in-a-row\}

A few short options can sit side by side. Keep the gap between options wider than the gap inside one, so each label belongs to its own circle.

<ComponentPreview name="row" height={104} title="In a row" background="plain" bodyClass="p-lg" html={`<fieldset class="fieldset">
  <legend class="fieldset-legend">Billing period</legend>
  <div class="flex flex-wrap gap-lg">
    <label class="check"><input type="radio" class="radio" name="period" checked> Monthly</label>
    <label class="check"><input type="radio" class="radio" name="period"> Yearly</label>
  </div>
</fieldset>`} />

## Disabled \{#disabled\}

A disabled radio fades, and its label drops to disabled ink. Disable one option, or the whole group by disabling its fieldset.

<ComponentPreview name="disabled" height={168} title="Disabled" background="plain" bodyClass="p-lg" html={`<fieldset class="fieldset">
  <legend class="fieldset-legend">Region</legend>
  <label class="check"><input type="radio" class="radio" name="region" checked> Tokyo</label>

  <label class="check"><input type="radio" class="radio" name="region" disabled> Virginia, at capacity</label>
</fieldset>`} />

## Choosing a control \{#choosing-a-control\}

Radios show every option at once and take a row each. Other controls make the same single choice in other shapes:

- A [segmented control](/components/segmented) for two to five short options in one row, such as a view switch.
- A [select](/components/select) when there are more options than fit, or space is tight.
- A [choice](/components/choice) when each option needs a title and a sentence.
- [Chips](/components/chip#one-of-several) for a filter that changes a view at once.

## Accessibility \{#accessibility\}

- Give every radio in a group the same `name`. Without it they do not exclude each other and the arrow keys do not move between them.
- A group has one tab stop. <kbd>Tab</kbd> reaches the chosen radio, or the first when none is chosen, and the arrow keys move from there.
- Start with one option chosen when there is a sensible default. A group with nothing chosen cannot be returned to that state by the reader.
