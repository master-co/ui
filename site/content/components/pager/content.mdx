A pager is the page before and the page after, at the foot of an article: two links side by side, each a flat face with a quiet word over the title of the page it leads to. The links say which is which with `rel="prev"` and `rel="next"`, and the stylesheet reads that, so there is no class for a direction.

## One attribute decides the side \{#rel\}

`rel` is the HTML attribute that says how a link relates to the page it is on, and `prev` and `next` are two of its values. A pager's link carries one of them, which is correct markup for a sequence in any case; the stylesheet reads it to do three things:

- The next page stands at the end of the row, with its words set to the end.
- Its arrow follows the word and points forward; the arrow of the page before leads the word and points back. Both turn round in a right-to-left page.
- A pager with only a next page keeps it at the end, where a reader's eye and thumb expect it.

Leave out the link that does not exist. The first page of a guide has only a next, and the last only a previous.

## Material and motion \{#material\}

A link is a flat face on one contour, with a large control's corner. Under the pointer it rises a step and its arrow moves 2px the way it points; pressed, it sinks into a well. With reduced motion the arrow stays where it is.

The pager is a size container. Under 26rem of its own width the two links stack, each as wide as the pager, the previous page first.

## Pager or pagination \{#pager-or-pagination\}

A pager moves between pages that are read in an order, and names them. [Pagination](/components/pagination) moves between the numbered pages of one long list. Where a page is in the site is a [breadcrumb](/components/breadcrumb).

## Accessibility \{#accessibility\}

- Put the links in a `<nav>` with an `aria-label`, so the landmark says what it is among the other navigation of the page.
- A link's name is its label and its title together: "Next, Theming". Keep the label; a title alone does not say which way it goes.
- The arrows are drawn by the stylesheet and are not announced.
