A breakout is a column at a reading measure, centred in the room it is given, that a picture, a table or a band can step out of: wider than the text by a little, or out to the edges. It is the layout of a long article, a changelog or a story, where the words keep a line that is comfortable to read and what is not words is allowed more room.

Everything inside reads in the column without a class. Only what leaves it says so, with `breakout-wide` or `breakout-full`. It draws no surface: a band that runs to the edges takes its face from a utility or a component.

## How it is built \{#how-it-is-built\}

- **One track.** The breakout is a grid whose only column is `--breakout-measure` wide, 40rem, or the room less a `--breakout-gutter` on each side, whichever is narrower. It is centred, and every child is placed in it without a class.
- **Leaving the column by its margins.** The breakout is an inline-size container. In a child, `100cqi` is the width of the breakout and `100%` is the width of the column, so the room beside the column is half their difference. `breakout-wide` takes that room with negative margins, up to `--breakout-wide` on each side and never into the gutter; `breakout-full` takes all of it.
- **A band whose words keep the column.** A full child that is also a `breakout` runs to the edges and sets its own children in a column at the same measure. The figures in the band above line up with the text over and under it.
- **A rhythm, not margins.** Children are 1.25rem apart, the row gap of the grid, because a grid does not collapse margins. A heading takes `mt-md` to stand a little further from what is above it than from what it introduces.
- **A wide table scrolls by itself.** It stands in a [scroll area](/components/scroll-area) with `scroll-area-inline`, which is `breakout-wide`, so on a phone it scrolls inside the column instead of widening the page.

## Wide and full \{#wide-and-full\}

How much a child gains depends on the room beside the column, and so on the width of the page:

| Room | The column | `breakout-wide` | `breakout-full` |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Wider than the measure and two wide reaches | 40rem, centred | 8rem more on each side: 56rem | The whole room |
| Between the two | 40rem, centred | What room there is, less the gutter | The whole room |
| Narrower than the measure | The room less two gutters | The same as the column | The whole room |

`--breakout-measure` is the widest the column may be. 40rem holds about 70 characters of body text; set it lower for larger type and higher for a column of code. `--breakout-wide` is how far a wide child may reach on each side, and `--breakout-gutter` is the least room kept between anything but a full child and an edge.

## A full band \{#full-band\}

A band that runs to the edges is usually a color or a picture with words on it, and the words should not run to the edges with it. Make the band a breakout of its own: it reaches the edges as a `breakout-full` child, and its own children are set in a column at the same measure as the text around it.

```html
<div class="breakout breakout-full py-xl bg-surface-tray">
  <p>These words line up with the column above and below the band.</p>
</div>
```

The band's color is a utility, `bg-surface-tray`, because the breakout draws nothing. A picture that runs to the edges is a `breakout-full` [figure](/components/figure) with `--figure-radius: 0`, since a corner at the edge of the window has nothing to turn against.

## When to use it \{#when\}

Use a breakout for a page that is one column of reading: an article, a post, a changelog, a guide. A page with regions beside the reading column, such as navigation and contents, is a [documentation page](/pages/docs), whose article column can itself be a breakout. A column that nothing ever leaves is written with utilities alone: `max-w(640) mx-auto` and a gutter.

## Accessibility \{#accessibility\}

- A breakout changes widths, never order: what is read first is what comes first in the markup.
- The measure is in rem, so it follows the reader's text size: a larger font makes a wider column of the same 70 or so characters, until the window is narrower than that, and then the column follows the window and nothing scrolls sideways.
- A table that is wider than the column scrolls in a [scroll area](/components/scroll-area) with a name and `tabindex="0"`, so a keyboard can scroll it.
