A text field is a well cut into its surface, edged by a half-pixel contour. On focus it rises to the sheet and takes a single ink contour with a soft halo. Use `.input` on `<input>` and `<textarea>`, and `.field` on the `<label>` that holds it.

<ComponentPreview name="default" title="Input" background="plain" bodyClass="p-lg" html={`<label class="field">
  Email
  <input class="input" type="email" placeholder="you@example.com">
</label>`} />

## Hint and error \{#hint-and-error\}

Connect a hint or an error to its field with `aria-describedby`, and mark an invalid value with `aria-invalid="true"`. A `field-error` shows only while its field is invalid, and replaces the hint while it shows, so both can be in the markup from the start.

<ComponentPreview name="hint" title="Hint and error" background="plain" bodyClass="grid gap-lg p-lg" html={`<label class="field">
  Project name
  <input class="input" value="atlas" aria-describedby="name-hint">
  <span class="field-hint" id="name-hint">Lowercase letters, numbers and dashes.</span>
</label>
<label class="field">
  Domain
  <input class="input" value="atlas" aria-invalid="true" aria-describedby="domain-error">
  <span class="field-error" id="domain-error">Enter a full domain, such as atlas.dev.</span>
</label>`} />

## Native validation \{#native-validation\}

The same states follow the browser's own constraints, without script. A field with `required`, a `type` or a `pattern` takes the error contour through `:user-invalid`, which matches only after the reader has edited the field or tried to submit, never on a fresh form. The `field-error` appears with it.

<ComponentPreview name="validation" title="Native validation" background="plain" bodyClass="p-lg" html={`<form class="flex flex-col gap-md">
  <label class="field">
    Work email
    <input class="input" type="email" required placeholder="you@company.com" aria-describedby="work-email-hint work-email-error">
    <span class="field-hint" id="work-email-hint">Type something that is not an email, then leave the field.</span>
    <span class="field-error" id="work-email-error">Enter an email address, such as ada@example.com.</span>
  </label>
  <button type="submit" class="btn btn-primary self-start">Continue</button>
</form>`} />

## Sizes \{#sizes\}

A field is 32, 38 or 44px tall: the heights of a [button](/components/button), a [select](/components/select) and a [segmented control](/components/segmented), so any of them share a row with no step between them. On touch screens every size is 44px.

<ComponentPreview name="sizes" title="Sizes" background="plain" bodyClass="grid gap-md p-lg" html={`<input class="input input-sm" placeholder="Small" aria-label="Small">
<input class="input" placeholder="Medium" aria-label="Medium">
<input class="input input-lg" placeholder="Large" aria-label="Large">`} />

## Textarea \{#textarea\}

A `<textarea>` with `.input` keeps the rows you give it and a vertical resize handle. Add `input-auto` to let it grow with its content instead: `field-sizing: content` sizes it from three lines up to twelve, then it scrolls. Set `--input-rows` and `--input-max-rows` to change the range. In a browser without `field-sizing` it keeps its minimum height and the handle.

<ComponentPreview name="textarea" title="Textarea" background="plain" bodyClass="grid gap-lg p-lg" html={`<label class="field">
  Message
  <textarea class="input" rows="3" placeholder="A fixed height with a resize handle"></textarea>
</label>
<label class="field">
  Notes
  <textarea class="input input-auto" placeholder="Keep typing: this one grows with its content"></textarea>
</label>`} />

## Input group \{#input-group\}

`input-group` is one well around a bare field and what belongs to it: an icon, a unit, a shortcut or a button. The group draws the hover, focus, invalid and disabled states of the field inside it through `:has()`; the field itself, an `input-group-field`, keeps the semantics and the caret. Make the group a `<label>` so the whole well focuses the field.

<ComponentPreview name="group" title="Input group" background="plain" bodyClass="grid gap-md p-lg" html={`<label class="input-group">
  <svg class="icon" viewBox="0 0 24 24" fill="none" stroke="currentColor" stroke-width="1.5" stroke-linecap="round" stroke-linejoin="round" aria-hidden="true"><circle cx="11" cy="11" r="6"/><path d="m20 20-4.35-4.35"/></svg>
  <input class="input-group-field" type="search" placeholder="Search projects" aria-label="Search projects">
  <kbd class="kbd">⌘K</kbd>
</label>
<label class="input-group">
  <span class="input-group-addon">https://</span>
  <input class="input-group-field" value="atlas" aria-label="Subdomain">
  <span class="input-group-addon">.master.co</span>
</label>
<label class="input-group">
  <input class="input-group-field" type="number" value="24" aria-label="Timeout">
  <span class="input-group-addon">seconds</span>
</label>`} />

A [button](/components/button) inside the group sits 4px from the well's edge and takes a corner 4px smaller than the well's, so the two stay concentric at every size. Use a `<div>` for the group when it holds a button.

<ComponentPreview name="inline" title="With a button" background="plain" bodyClass="grid gap-md p-lg" html={`<form class="input-group">
  <input class="input-group-field" type="email" placeholder="you@example.com" aria-label="Email">
  <button type="submit" class="btn btn-primary">Subscribe</button>
</form>
<div class="input-group input-group-sm">
  <input class="input-group-field" value="https://atlas.dev/p/9f2c" readonly aria-label="Share link">
  <button type="button" class="btn">Copy</button>
</div>`} />

To keep a field and a button apart instead, put them in a flex row: `<form class="flex gap-xs">`.

## Several values \{#several-values\}

`input-group-wrap` turns the group into a field that collects several values: recipients, tags, filters. Each value is a [chip](/components/chip) in the well, the field takes what is left of the last line, and the well grows by a line when the values need one. A chip sits 6px in from the well's edge and takes the concentric corner.

The stylesheet lays the values out; adding one on <kbd class="kbd">Enter</kbd> and removing the last on <kbd class="kbd">Backspace</kbd> is a few lines of your own script, or a round trip to the server.

<ComponentPreview name="wrap" title="Several values" background="plain" bodyClass="p-lg" html={`<div class="field max-w(420) mx-auto">
  <label for="input-teams">Teams</label>
  <div class="input-group input-group-wrap">
    <span class="chip">Design<button type="button" class="chip-remove" aria-label="Remove Design"></button></span>
    <span class="chip">Research<button type="button" class="chip-remove" aria-label="Remove Research"></button></span>
    <span class="chip">Engineering<button type="button" class="chip-remove" aria-label="Remove Engineering"></button></span>
    <span class="chip">Operations<button type="button" class="chip-remove" aria-label="Remove Operations"></button></span>
    <input class="input-group-field" id="input-teams" placeholder="Add a team">
  </div>
</div>`} />

## Search and autofill \{#search-and-autofill\}

A `type="search"` field keeps the browser's clear button, redrawn as a fine cross in the ink of an icon. When the browser fills a field in, the well takes a faint tint of the info color in place of the engine's own yellow or blue, so a filled form still looks like this one.

<ComponentPreview name="search" title="Search" background="plain" bodyClass="p-lg" html={`<input class="input max-w(360) mx-auto" type="search" value="moonstone" aria-label="Search">`} caption="Hover or focus the field to see its clear button in Chromium and Safari." />

On `<input type="file">` the browser's button becomes a small raised control that sits in the well like an option in a segmented track.

  Attachment

## Date, time, number and color \{#date-time-and-color\}

`.input` draws the browser's own date, time, number and color fields in the same well. The picker stays the platform's, which is the one a reader already knows on their device. A color field becomes a square whose swatch sits 4px inside, with a concentric corner.

The small marks a browser puts in these fields are redrawn at the weight of an icon and in its ink: a calendar or a clock that opens the picker, and a pair of chevrons that step a number, the upper one up and the lower one down. Chromium and Safari expose those parts; Firefox keeps its own. For a number a reader steps often, use a [stepper](/components/stepper).

<ComponentPreview name="native" title="Date, time, number and color" background="plain" bodyClass="grid grid-cols(2) gap-md p-lg" html={`<label class="field">
  Starts
  <input class="input" type="date" value="2026-11-11">
</label>
<label class="field">
  At
  <input class="input" type="time" value="09:30">
</label>
<label class="field">
  Timeout in seconds
  <input class="input" type="number" min="0" step="5" value="30">
</label>
<label class="field">
  Accent
  <input class="input" type="color" value="#5b4fe0">
</label>`} />

## Fieldset \{#fieldset\}

`fieldset` on a `<fieldset>` groups related controls under one caption without the browser's groove, and `fieldset-legend` gives the `<legend>` the voice of a field label. Use it around [checkboxes](/components/checkbox), [radios](/components/radio) and [choices](/components/choice), so the group has a name.

<ComponentPreview name="fieldset" title="Fieldset" background="plain" bodyClass="p-lg" html={`<fieldset class="fieldset">
  <legend class="fieldset-legend">Notify me about</legend>
  <label class="check"><input type="checkbox" class="checkbox" checked> Failed deployments</label>
  <label class="check"><input type="checkbox" class="checkbox" checked> Expiring certificates</label>
  <label class="check"><input type="checkbox" class="checkbox"> Product news</label>
</fieldset>`} />
