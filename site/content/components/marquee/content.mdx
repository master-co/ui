A marquee is a row that passes by without end: the names of customers under a hero, a line of quotes, a ticker. It has no surface and no edge of its own; both ends fade into whatever is behind it. It runs on one CSS animation.

## Two copies of the row \{#two-copies\}

The row is written twice, each copy a `marquee-track`. Both travel one track's length and start again, so the second copy is always where the first one was and the seam never shows. Mark the second copy `aria-hidden="true"`: it exists for the eye, and a screen reader should hear the row once.

A track is at least as wide as the marquee and spreads its items along itself, so a row of three names still reaches both ends.

## Stopping \{#stopping\}

The row stops while the pointer is over it or the keyboard focus is inside it, which lets a reader finish a quote or follow a link. That is the pause WCAG asks of anything that moves for more than five seconds beside other content.

With `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` nothing travels at all. The second copy is removed, the fade is dropped, and the first track wraps onto as many centered lines as it needs: the same content, standing still.

## Speed, spacing and direction \{#speed-spacing-direction\}

`--marquee-duration` is the time one copy takes to pass, 40 seconds by default. A longer row needs a longer time to move at the same pace. `--marquee-gap` is the space between items and `--marquee-fade` the length of the fade at each end. `marquee-reverse` sends the row the other way, for the second of two rows.

## When not to use it \{#when-not-to-use-it\}

Moving text is hard to read and easy to resent. Use a marquee for content a reader may glance at and lose nothing by missing: names, short praise, a decorative ticker. Anything a reader must find, compare or act on belongs in a [carousel](/components/carousel), which they move themselves, or in a plain row.

## Accessibility \{#accessibility\}

- Give the marquee `role="group"` and an `aria-label` that says what the row is.
- Hide the second track with `aria-hidden="true"`, and keep links and buttons out of it or give them `tabindex="-1"`, so focus never lands on a copy.
- Do not put information here that appears nowhere else on the page.

## Classes \{#classes\}

| Class | Description |
| --- | --- |
| `marquee` | The row: clips, fades both ends, stops on hover and focus |
| `marquee-reverse` | Passes the other way |
| `marquee-track` | One copy of the row; write it twice |

## Custom properties \{#custom-properties\}

| Property | Default | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `--marquee-duration` | `40s` | Time for one copy to pass |
| `--marquee-gap` | `3rem` | Space between items, and between the copies |
| `--marquee-fade` | `4rem` | Length of the fade at each end |
