A breadcrumb shows where the current page sits and gives a way back up. It is an ordered list of links inside a `<nav>`: quiet ink for the way back, strong ink for where you are, and a small chevron the stylesheet draws between steps.

<ComponentPreview name="default" title="Breadcrumb" background="plain" html={`<nav aria-label="Breadcrumb">
  <ol class="breadcrumb">
    <li class="breadcrumb-item"><a class="breadcrumb-link" href="#projects">Projects</a></li>
    <li class="breadcrumb-item"><a class="breadcrumb-link" href="#atlas">Atlas</a></li>
    <li class="breadcrumb-item"><a class="breadcrumb-link" href="#deployments" aria-current="page">Deployments</a></li>
  </ol>
</nav>`} />

## Long paths \{#long-paths\}

Steps wrap onto a second line when the row runs out, and a single long name ends in an ellipsis at 16rem. To shorten a deep path, replace the middle steps with a [menu](/components/menu).

## Accessibility \{#accessibility\}

- Wrap the list in `<nav aria-label="Breadcrumb">` and use an `<ol>`: the order is the meaning.
- Mark the last step with `aria-current="page"`. It may stay a link to the current page or be an `<a>` without `href`.
- The chevrons are drawn by the stylesheet and are not announced. They mirror in right-to-left text.
