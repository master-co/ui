A trace shows what an agent or a job did, step by step: what it thought, searched, read and ran. Each step is one quiet line with a marker on a column, and a hairline joins the markers. A step with something behind it is a native `<details>`, so its line opens the detail with no script; a step with nothing behind it is a plain line.

## A step \{#a-step\}

A step is a `trace-step` around a `trace-summary`: the marker, the words, and an optional `trace-meta` at the end of the line for a fact in figures, such as how long it took or how many it found.

When the step has a detail, write it as a `<details>` with the line on its `<summary>` and the detail in `trace-detail` after it. The stylesheet sees the detail and turns the line into a toggle: a small chevron follows the words and points down while the step is open. The detail opens to its own height where the browser can transition `::details-content`, and at once elsewhere. Find-in-page opens a closed step that holds a match, as it does for any `<details>`.

## Markers \{#markers\}

An empty `trace-marker` is a small dot. A marker may hold an [icon](/components/icon) instead, to say what kind of step it was, or a [spinner](/components/spinner) with `spinner-inline` while the step runs.

## Running and failed \{#running-and-failed\}

The step under way carries `aria-busy="true"`: its words take the strong ink and a bare dot sends out a ring, which rests as a still halo for a reader who asks for reduced motion. Remove the attribute when the step ends.

A step that failed is `trace-failed`: its marker and the fact at the end of its line take the danger ink. It is a class because ARIA has no word for a failed item of a list. Say what failed in words, in the line or behind it, so the color is never the only sign.

## One line \{#one-line\}

A trace of one step is the line an assistant shows above its answer: what it did, and how long it took, with the reasoning behind it.

## Trace, timeline or steps \{#trace-timeline-or-steps\}

A trace is work: its steps may be running, may have failed, and open to show what stands behind them. A [timeline](/components/timeline) is a record of events with their times. [Steps](/components/steps) are a fixed sequence a reader moves through.

## Accessibility \{#accessibility\}

- A step that opens is a `<details>`: the browser gives its line the role, the state and the keys of a disclosure.
- The marker is drawn by the stylesheet and is not announced. An icon in it is decoration: say what the step was in the line.
- A trace that grows while it is on screen should sit in a region with `aria-live="polite"`, so each new step is announced once it is added. `aria-busy` on the running step marks it for assistive technology as well as for the eye.
- On a touch screen every line is 44px tall.
