A combobox is a text field that opens a [listbox](/components/listbox) of matches: a select you can type into. It is made of parts you already have, an [input group](/components/input#input-group) and a floating listbox, inside a `combobox` wrapper that ties the two together. The stylesheet places and draws it; the `combobox` [behavior](/guide/behaviors) filters the options, moves through them and writes the chosen one into the field.

## What the stylesheet does \{#what-the-stylesheet-does\}

- **Placement.** The `combobox` wrapper names itself as an anchor and scopes the name to itself. Its listbox opens 6px under the whole field, not under the caret or the button, and takes at least the field's width through `anchor-size()`. Nothing is measured, and several comboboxes on a page do not interfere.
- **The chevron.** `combobox-toggle` is a small button at the field's end. Its chevron turns over while the field says `aria-expanded="true"`.
- **The empty state.** `listbox-empty` appears by itself once every option is `hidden`.
- **The field's states.** Hover, focus, invalid and disabled come from the input group.

In a browser without anchor positioning the listbox still opens, centered in the viewport as any popover is.

## The behavior \{#behavior\}

`combobox` takes the root, the element with the `combobox` class, and returns the function that detaches it. It has no options. It finds the field by `role="combobox"` and the list by `role="listbox"`.

```js name=app.js
import { combobox } from '@master/ui/behaviors/combobox.js'

const detach = combobox(document.querySelector('.combobox'))
```

- **Typing** sets `hidden` on the options whose text does not hold what is typed, whatever its case, opens the list and makes the first match the active option.
- **The active option** has `aria-selected="true"` and is named by `aria-activedescendant` on the field. Focus stays in the field.
- **Choosing**, with <kbd>Enter</kbd> or the pointer, writes the option's text into the field, without its `listbox-description` and `listbox-end`, marks the option `aria-checked="true"` and the others `false`, and closes the list. Typing over a chosen value takes its check away.
- **Opening and closing.** The listbox is `popover="manual"`, so typing and clicking in the field do not dismiss it. The behavior opens it on a click in the field, on the toggle and on the arrow keys, closes it on <kbd>Esc</kbd> and when focus leaves the combobox, and keeps `aria-expanded` in step.
- **Events.** The field dispatches `input`, then `change`, when an option is chosen, as it does for typing. The value is the field's `value`.

Disabled and hidden options are skipped. While an input method composes, <kbd>Enter</kbd> and the arrows belong to it.

Without the behavior the field is a text input: the reader can type a value, and the list does not open.

## Choosing a control \{#choosing-a-control\}

For up to a dozen plain options, a [select](/components/select) does this with no script and the platform's own type-ahead. Reach for a combobox when the list is long enough to search, loads as the reader types, or has rich rows.

## Keyboard \{#keyboard\}

The keys are those of the behavior. With the list closed, and with <kbd>Shift</kbd> held, <kbd>Home</kbd> and <kbd>End</kbd> move the caret as in any field. <kbd>Enter</kbd> without an active option is left alone, so it still submits a form.

## Accessibility \{#accessibility\}

- The input has `role="combobox"`, `aria-controls` pointing at the listbox, `aria-autocomplete="list"` and `aria-expanded`.
- Focus stays in the input; the active option is announced through `aria-activedescendant`.
- Take the toggle button out of the tab order with `tabindex="-1"`: the arrow keys already open the list from the field.
- Set `autocomplete="off"` so the browser's own suggestions do not cover the list.
