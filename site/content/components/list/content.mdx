A list is rows of things, each with something to recognize it by, its words, and something at its end: members, files, integrations, settings. Rows sit on hairlines that start at the text, not under the media, so the column of avatars or icons reads as one edge. A list has no surface of its own; put it in a [card](/components/card) or a [sheet](/components/surface#sheet).

## Parts \{#parts\}

- `list-media` is what the row is recognized by: an [avatar](/components/avatar), an [icon](/components/icon) or a thumbnail. It keeps its size.
- `list-body` holds a `list-title` and an optional `list-description`. Each stays on one line and ends in an ellipsis when the row is narrow.
- `list-end` is a value, a status or a control at the end of the row.

The hairline under a row starts where the body starts. Where the browser supports anchor positioning, the body names itself as an anchor scoped to its row and the line is drawn from that edge; elsewhere the line spans the row.

## Interactive \{#interactive\}

`list-interactive` is for rows that open or select: put `list-row` on the `<a>` or `<button>` itself. Each row becomes a rounded target with room at its sides and answers the pointer at once; the hairlines step aside, since the rows now part themselves. The current row has `aria-current`.

## Choosing a component \{#choosing-a-component\}

- A [table](/components/table) when rows are compared column by column.
- A [nav](/components/nav) for a short list of destinations with icons and no second line.
- A [listbox](/components/listbox) for options chosen with the keys.

## Accessibility \{#accessibility\}

- Use a `<ul>` or an `<ol>`, so the number of rows is announced.
- When the name is written in the row, the avatar beside it is decoration: `alt=""` or `aria-hidden="true"`.
- A row that is one link must not hold other controls. Give a row with its own controls, such as a switch or a menu button, a plain `<li>`.
