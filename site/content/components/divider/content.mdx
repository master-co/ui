A divider is one hairline between sections. Use `.divider` on an `<hr>`, which already carries the separator role. Reach for it after spacing and grouping have done what they can: a line is the strongest way to part two things.

<ComponentPreview name="default" height={128} narrowHeight={168} title="Divider" background="plain" bodyClass="flex flex-col gap-md p-lg" html={`<p class="m(0) text-sm fg-text-body">Billing is managed by the workspace owner.</p>
<hr class="divider">
<p class="m(0) text-sm fg-text-body">Invoices are sent on the first day of each month.</p>`} />

## With a label \{#with-a-label\}

`divider-label` parts the line around a caption. Put it on an element with `role="separator"`, since an `<hr>` cannot hold text.

<ComponentPreview name="label" height={192} title="With a label" background="plain" bodyClass="flex flex-col gap-md p-lg" html={`<button type="button" class="btn w-full">Continue with a passkey</button>
<div class="divider divider-label" role="separator">or</div>
<input class="input" type="email" placeholder="you@example.com" aria-label="Email">`} />

## Vertical \{#vertical\}

`divider-vertical` stands between items in a row and takes the row's height. Give it `role="separator"` and `aria-orientation="vertical"` when it parts groups of controls; hide it with `aria-hidden="true"` when it is only visual.

<ComponentPreview name="vertical" height={96} title="Vertical" background="plain" html={`<div class="flex items-center gap-sm">
  <button type="button" class="btn btn-quiet btn-sm">Edit</button>
  <span class="divider divider-vertical" role="separator" aria-orientation="vertical"></span>
  <button type="button" class="btn btn-quiet btn-sm">Share</button>
  <button type="button" class="btn btn-quiet btn-sm">Export</button>
</div>`} />
