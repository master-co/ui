A citation is a small numbered mark at the end of a claim that points at where the claim came from. It is a link, drawn as a chip-toned pill a little smaller than the text and raised a little above the baseline. Under the pointer, and while its source is showing, it fills with ink.

## Where it points \{#where-it-points\}

There are two honest targets for a citation, and both need no script:

- **A list of sources**, usually at the foot of the answer or the article. The mark is an `<a>` whose `href` is the entry's id.
- **A [popover](/components/popover)** with the source's title and where it is from. The mark is a `<button>` with `popovertarget`, written directly before its popover, and it stays filled while the popover is open. Inside a paragraph the popover is a `<span>`, since a paragraph cannot hold a `<div>`.

Write the mark with no space before it. It is an inline element, so it wraps with the word it follows and never starts a line by itself.

## Accessibility \{#accessibility\}

- A bare number is not a name. Give each mark an `aria-label` such as "Source 2", or the source's title.
- Keep the sources reachable without the marks: a list a reader can scan is more use than twelve popovers.
- The mark is small. Its number is set at the control weight in tabular figures, and its target grows with the text it sits in.

## Classes \{#classes\}

| Class | Description |
| --- | --- |
| `citation` | The mark, on an `<a>` or a `<button>`. `aria-current="true"` or an open popover right after it fills it with ink |
