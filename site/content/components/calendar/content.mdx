A calendar is a month of days to pick from. It is an ARIA grid of buttons: the stylesheet draws every state, and the `calendar` [behavior](/guide/behaviors) supplies the dates, the month buttons and the arrow keys. It has no surface of its own, so it sits in a [card](/components/card), or in a [popover](/components/popover) to make a date picker.

The markup is the header and an empty grid. The behavior writes the month's name into `calendar-title`, and the weekday names and the weeks into `calendar-grid`; it opens on the month of today. Without the script the grid stays as the markup has it, so a calendar that must read before the script runs is rendered on the server with its weeks, as the [range](#range) below is written, and the behavior replaces them when it attaches.

## States \{#states\}

Every state is an attribute or a class on the day's button. The behavior sets them; markup written by hand or on a server sets the same ones.

| State | Mark it with | Drawn as |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Today | `aria-current="date"` | A dot under the number |
| Chosen | `aria-selected="true"` | Ink, the way a checked [checkbox](/components/checkbox) fills |
| Outside the month | `calendar-day-outside` | Muted ink |
| Unavailable | `disabled` | Disabled ink |

A day answers the pointer at once, like a row in a [menu](/components/menu). Today keeps its dot when it is chosen, in the ink of the day.

## Range \{#range\}

A range is two ink days joined by a chip-toned band. The first day has `calendar-day-range-start`, the last `calendar-day-range-end` and the days between `calendar-day-range`. All of them are `aria-selected="true"`.

This preview is markup only, with no script attached, so the marks can be read in its Code view: a whole month as the behavior writes it, with a range, today and two days that cannot be chosen.

The band is drawn so it reads as one shape:

- Columns have no gap, so the band runs from cell to cell. Rows keep 2px apart, so each week is its own band.
- The band rounds where a week ends and the range goes on.
- At each end the band covers the half of the cell that faces the range, and the ink day sits on it as a layer of its own. The band therefore meets the day's middle, not its rounded edge.

With the `range` option the behavior chooses a range: the first click is its start, the second its end, and a third starts again. When the second day is the earlier one, the two are put in order.

## Date picker \{#date-picker\}

A date picker is a field, a button and a calendar in a [popover](/components/popover). The popover opens and closes without script and places itself against its button. The behavior draws the calendar and says which day is chosen; the page's own script writes that day into the field and hides the popover.

The calendar's `change` event bubbles, so the listener is on the popover. Its `detail.value` is the day as `YYYY-MM-DD`, which is also the value a form expects of a date.

On a phone, or when a plain date is all the form needs, a native [`<input type="date">`](/components/input#date-time-and-color) gives the reader the picker their device already has.

## The behavior \{#behavior\}

`calendar` takes the root and an object of options, and returns the function that detaches it.

```js name=app.js
import { calendar } from '@master/ui/behaviors/calendar.js'

const root = document.querySelector('.calendar')
const picker = calendar(root, { value: '2026-11-04', min: '2026-11-01', max: '2026-12-31' })

root.addEventListener('change', (event) => console.log(event.detail.value))
```

| Option | What it sets |
| --- | --- |
| `value` | The chosen day as `YYYY-MM-DD`, or the two ends of a range |
| `month` | The month to show first, as `YYYY-MM`. By default the month of the value, or of today |
| `min`, `max` | The first and the last day that can be chosen, as `YYYY-MM-DD`. A month button is disabled when there is no month to go to |
| `range` | `true` to choose two days and every day between them |
| `weekStart` | The day in the first column, 0 for Sunday to 6 for Saturday. By default the locale's own, and Monday where the browser cannot say |
| `locale` | A BCP 47 tag for the names of months and days. By default the `lang` the calendar stands in |
| `disabled` | A function that takes a `Date` and says whether that day cannot be chosen |

- **What it writes.** The weekday names and the weeks of the shown month, and the month's name. On the days: `aria-current="date"` on today, `aria-selected="true"` and the range classes on what is chosen, `disabled` on a day that cannot be chosen, a full date as `aria-label`, and `tabindex` so one day is in the tab order.
- **The event.** Choosing a day dispatches a bubbling `change` from the root with `detail.value`: the day as `YYYY-MM-DD`, or `[start, end]` once both ends of a range are chosen.
- **The value.** It lives in the script, not in the markup. Read it from the returned function's `value`: `picker.value`. In a range the end is `null` until it is chosen. To set it from outside, attach again with a new `value`; the second call replaces the first.
- **Days.** They are local and Gregorian.

## Structure \{#structure\}

- `calendar-grid` declares seven columns once. Each `calendar-week` is a row on a subgrid of them, so the weekday names and the days share the same columns without a table.
- `calendar-weekday` is a column heading. Its `aria-label` is the full name.
- `calendar-day` is a `<button>` with `role="gridcell"`.

## Keyboard \{#keyboard\}

The behavior gives the grid the keys of a date grid. A move past the edge of the month shows the neighboring month and keeps focus on the new day. A move never lands on a day that cannot be chosen, and stops at `min` and `max`.

## Accessibility \{#accessibility\}

- The grid is `role="grid"`, labelled by the month's title; each week is `role="row"`.
- One day is in the tab order: the chosen one, or today, or the first day of a month that holds neither. The rest have `tabindex="-1"`.
- Give the title `aria-live="polite"`, so a change of month is announced.
- On touch screens a day is 44px.
