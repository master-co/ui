An accordion is a list of rows that open in place. Each row is a native `<details>` with a `<summary>`, so the browser keeps the toggle, the keys and find-in-page, which opens a closed row when it holds a match. The stylesheet draws the rows on hairlines and lets each panel open to its own height.

<ComponentPreview name="default" height={256} narrowHeight={320} title="Accordion" background="plain" bodyClass="p-lg" html={`<div class="accordion">
  <details class="accordion-item" name="faq" open>
    <summary class="accordion-trigger">Does it need JavaScript?</summary>
    <div class="accordion-content">No. Every component is a native element with one stylesheet; the browser keeps its behavior.</div>
  </details>
  <details class="accordion-item" name="faq">
    <summary class="accordion-trigger">Can I change the radius?</summary>
    <div class="accordion-content">Yes. Override the radius tokens after importing Master UI, or set a component's own property such as --btn-radius.</div>
  </details>
  <details class="accordion-item" name="faq">
    <summary class="accordion-trigger">Is dark mode a second stylesheet?</summary>
    <div class="accordion-content">No. Every color token resolves with light-dark(), following the page's color scheme.</div>
  </details>
</div>`} />

## One at a time, or many \{#one-at-a-time-or-many\}

Rows that share a `name` attribute form a group: opening one closes the others, as above. Leave `name` out to let any number stay open.

## On a surface \{#on-a-surface\}

The accordion has no material of its own. Put it in a [card](/components/card) or a [sheet](/components/surface#sheet) for a boxed list.

## Motion \{#motion\}

The panel is the `::details-content` pseudo-element. `interpolate-size: allow-keywords` lets its `block-size` travel from `0` to `auto`, and `content-visibility` transitions with `allow-discrete` so the text stays painted until the panel has closed. The chevron turns over with it. In a browser without these the panel opens at once; with reduced motion it fades in place.
