A rating is a score out of a few stars. It is a group of native radios, each drawn as a star in ink, so the arrow keys, the form value and assistive technology work as they do for any radio group.

## How the stars fill \{#how-the-stars-fill\}

The radios are in reading order, one to five. `:has()` fills every star that has the checked one after it, and the checked one itself: `.rating-input:has(~ .rating-input:checked)`. The same selector with `:hover` previews the score under the pointer. Before `:has()`, this needed the stars in reverse order and a reversed row, which broke the keys.

## Sizes \{#sizes\}

Stars are 20px. `rating-sm` is 16px, for a row in a list, and `rating-lg` is 24px. On touch screens each star is at least 28px.

## Read only \{#read-only\}

To show a score, disable the radios: the stars keep their ink and stop answering the pointer. Say the score in words beside them, since a row of stars is not a number.

## Accessibility \{#accessibility\}

- Name the group with `aria-label` or a `<legend>`, and each radio with its score.
- A star is cut with a mask, which would also cut its own focus ring, so the group carries the ring while one of its radios has keyboard focus.
- Stars are ink, not yellow: the interface keeps hue for status.
