Steps show where a reader is in a short sequence, such as a checkout or a setup. They are an ordered list whose markers are numbered by a CSS counter. Only the current step is marked, with `aria-current="step"`; the stylesheet works out the rest.

<ComponentPreview name="default" height={96} title="Steps" background="plain" bodyClass="p-lg" html={`<ol class="steps">
  <li class="steps-item"><span class="steps-title">Account</span></li>
  <li class="steps-item" aria-current="step"><span class="steps-title">Billing</span></li>
  <li class="steps-item"><span class="steps-title">Confirm</span></li>
</ol>`} />

## One attribute \{#one-attribute\}

Three states come from one attribute:

- The **current** step has `aria-current="step"`. Its marker rises as a small sheet with an ink contour.
- A step is **complete** when the current one comes after it: `.steps-item:has(~ .steps-item[aria-current="step"])`. Its marker is an ink disc with the check cut out of it, so the mark is the surface showing through and reads in both themes, and the line leading on from it turns to ink.
- Every other step **waits** in a well, with its number.

Move the attribute to the next item and all three update. When the whole sequence is done, no step is current: add `steps-complete` to the list and every step is complete.

<ComponentPreview name="complete" height={96} title="Complete" background="plain" bodyClass="p-lg" html={`<ol class="steps steps-complete">
  <li class="steps-item"><span class="steps-title">Account</span></li>
  <li class="steps-item"><span class="steps-title">Billing</span></li>
  <li class="steps-item"><span class="steps-title">Confirm</span></li>
</ol>`} />

## Vertical \{#vertical\}

`steps-vertical` sets one step under another, for a narrow column or for steps that carry a sentence each. Wrap the words in `steps-body` and add a `steps-description`.

## As links \{#as-links\}

When a reader may go back, make the title of a complete step a [link](/components/link). Steps that wait stay plain text.

<ComponentPreview name="links" height={96} title="As links" background="plain" bodyClass="p-lg" html={`<nav aria-label="Checkout">
  <ol class="steps">
    <li class="steps-item"><a class="steps-title link link-quiet" href="#cart">Cart</a></li>
    <li class="steps-item"><a class="steps-title link link-quiet" href="#shipping">Shipping</a></li>
    <li class="steps-item" aria-current="step"><span class="steps-title">Payment</span></li>
    <li class="steps-item"><span class="steps-title">Review</span></li>
  </ol>
</nav>`} />

## Accessibility \{#accessibility\}

- Use an `<ol>`: the order is the meaning, and the list announces how many steps there are.
- `aria-current="step"` tells assistive technology which step is current. The check and the ink are visual only, so the list order carries completeness for a screen reader.
- Keep horizontal steps to four or five short titles. Past that, use `steps-vertical`.
