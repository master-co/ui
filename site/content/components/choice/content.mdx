A choice is an option large enough to explain itself: a plan, a payment method, a setting with a sentence under it. It is a `<label>` around a native [radio](/components/radio) or [checkbox](/components/checkbox), so the whole face is the target and the input keeps the state and the keys. At rest it is a flat face on one contour; chosen, it rises one step and its contour turns to ink.

## How it reads its state \{#how-it-reads-its-state\}

The choice has no state of its own. It asks the input inside it with `:has()`: `:has(:checked)` raises the face, `:has(:focus-visible)` draws the focus ring around the whole option instead of around the small input, and `:has(:disabled)` drops it to a well. Nothing is toggled by script and nothing can fall out of step with the form.

## Accessibility \{#accessibility\}

- Group choices in a [`fieldset`](/components/input#fieldset) with a legend, and give radios one `name`.
- The title and the description are both part of the label, so both are read as the option's name. Keep the description to one sentence.
- The chosen state is drawn with ink and depth and by the input's own mark, never by hue.
