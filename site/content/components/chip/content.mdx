A chip is a small pill that is a choice or a value. Its ends are true half circles, which tells it apart from a [badge](/components/badge): a badge describes, a chip can be acted on. As a filter, a chip is a `<label>` around a native checkbox or radio, raised at rest and filled with ink when chosen, the way a [checkbox](/components/checkbox) fills.

## One of several \{#one-of-several\}

With radios that share a `name`, chips are a single choice, and the arrow keys move between them. Use this for a short set that changes a view at once; for a value in a form, a [segmented control](/components/segmented) reads as one control.

## Values \{#values\}

Without an input a chip is a value: a recipient, a tag, an applied filter. It rests in a chip tone on one contour. `chip-remove` is a round button at its end; give it a name that includes the value. Put [`icon`](/components/icon) on a leading icon.

## Pictures, dots and counts \{#parts\}

A chip may hold, in this order, a picture (`chip-media`, an [avatar](/components/avatar) or an icon it sizes and rounds), a category dot (`chip-dot`), its words (`chip-label`), a count (a [badge](/components/badge) with `chip-count`) and a remove button. The words give way first and end in an ellipsis; the picture, the count and the button keep their size. A picture at the start and a count at the end stand as far from the chip's end as from its top and bottom, so the curves are concentric.

A filter chip is a label around its input, so the whole chip is the target: keep a remove button out of it. A value that can be removed is a plain chip with a `chip-remove`.

## Category \{#category\}

`chip-category` reads the [category](/components/category) set on the chip or around it. A value takes the category's surface, text and line. A filter keeps its raised face, and its ink when chosen, so the choice reads the same in every category; its `chip-dot` carries the category.

## Sizes \{#sizes\}

`chip-sm` is 24px and `chip-lg` 32px; the default is 28px. The remove button, the picture and the count scale with it. On a touch screen a chip keeps its height: a filter's target reaches 44px around the pill, and a remove button's reaches 44px tall within its own value. A field that holds values, such as [tags](/components/tags), sets their height for itself.

## Accessibility \{#accessibility\}

- The input inside a filter chip covers it invisibly, so the label is the target and the input keeps the state, the keys and the form value. Group filter chips in a `<fieldset>` with a `<legend>`, or a `role="radiogroup"` with a label.
- The remove button's target reaches past its glyph without moving it: 28px, and 44px tall on touch screens, never past the end of its own value.
- A category dot is decoration: the chip's words name the category.
- A chosen chip is ink on moonstone and pearl on obsidian, with no hue, so it does not depend on color vision.
