## Tab panels \{#tab-panels\}

For in-page panels, follow the WAI-ARIA tabs pattern: a `role="tablist"` container, `role="tab"` buttons with `aria-selected` and `aria-controls`, and `role="tabpanel"` regions. The stylesheet covers the visual states; switching panels and arrow-key focus need a small script, which the upcoming framework bindings will provide.

<ComponentPreview name="tablist" title="Tab list" bodyClass="p-lg" html={`<div class="tabs" role="tablist" aria-label="Account">
  <button type="button" class="tab" role="tab" id="tab-profile" aria-selected="true" aria-controls="panel-profile">Profile</button>
  <button type="button" class="tab" role="tab" id="tab-billing" aria-selected="false" aria-controls="panel-billing" tabindex="-1">Billing</button>
</div>
<div role="tabpanel" id="panel-profile" aria-labelledby="tab-profile" class="pt-md text-sm fg-text-body">Profile settings</div>`} />
